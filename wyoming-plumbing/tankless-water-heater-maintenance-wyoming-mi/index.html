    Thorough Tankless Water Heater Maintenance Services in Wyoming, MI

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Wyoming

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Wyoming

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Wyoming

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Wyoming homes.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Service in Wyoming

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Wyoming, MI

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.
