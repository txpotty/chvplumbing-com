    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Wyoming, MI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Wyoming

    Electric water heaters in Wyoming usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Wyoming

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.
