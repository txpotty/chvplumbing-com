    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Wyoming

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Wyoming, MI

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Look for screwdriver stops behind the trim plate or an access panel behind the tub, and if neither is there, close the main shutoff and leave the tub drain open so it does not overflow. A plumber heads out as soon as one is free. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Wyoming

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Wyoming

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. A new valve makes more sense.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Wyoming, MI

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. Matching the sink faucet is easy. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.
