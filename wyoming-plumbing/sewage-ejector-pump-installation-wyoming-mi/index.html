    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wyoming

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wyoming, MI

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wyoming

    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Wyoming

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wyoming, MI

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wyoming

    Residential ejector systems in Wyoming serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.
