    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.
