    PEX Pipe Installation in Wyoming, MI

    PEX piping has become a popular choice for new plumbing installations and repipes in Wyoming, MI due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion. CHV Plumbing installs PEX piping for water supply lines, offering a cost-effective and long-lasting alternative to copper. Its ability to expand makes it more resistant to freeze damage than rigid pipe. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. We make sure all connections are secure and leak-free.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Wyoming, MI

    For endless hot water on demand and energy savings, consider an electric tankless water heater installation from CHV Plumbing in Wyoming, MI. We handle all plumbing connections, setting proper flow rates and temperature settings for consistent hot water.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Wyoming, MI

    Water heater expansion tanks absorb thermal expansion in closed systems, which protects pipes and relief valves. Homes with a pressure reducing valve often need one.     PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Wyoming

    PEX is flexible, resistant to scale and a popular choice for repiping older homes with failing copper or galvanized pipe. We install PEX with manifold or home-run layouts for balanced pressure. The material handles hard water well and can reduce noise in the walls. We patch access openings neatly or coordinate with your drywall contractor. A PEX repipe can stop recurring pinhole leaks.
