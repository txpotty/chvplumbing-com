Our plumbers quickly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs.     Control Valve Repair in Wyoming

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. CHV Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, restoring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Wyoming.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Wyoming

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Wyoming

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.
