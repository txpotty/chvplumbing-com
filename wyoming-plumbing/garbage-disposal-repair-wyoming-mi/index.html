    Garbage Disposal Problems We Fix in Wyoming, MI

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Wyoming

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Wyoming, MI

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Wyoming

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Wyoming

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Wyoming, MI

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Wyoming

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Most of the time the flywheel is stuck. Shut off the power and keep your hand out, because the ring is sharp and the reset can restart the motor. We can free it safely.

    Clearing a jam with a hex key or pressing the reset is safe with the power off. For leaks that keep coming back, wiring trouble or a failing motor, a plumber is the safer and faster option. Keep your hands out of the chamber.

    One to two hours covers most repairs. Jams and resets often take under 30 minutes, while a flange reseal means dropping the unit, cleaning the old putty and setting the flange again, which takes longer.

    Most early failures start with stringy or hard scraps. Bones, pits, celery, corn husks and non-food items jam the impellers, and running hot water with grease coats the chamber and lets scale build faster in hard water areas. Power surges can also hurt motors.

    Use cold water while grinding, and run the unit every few days even if there is little in it, which keeps the impellers moving and helps prevent rust. Feed scraps in slowly instead of packing the chamber, and keep grease, fibrous foods and coffee grounds out of it. Citrus peels help the smell.

    Frequent jams, constant resets, leaks from the body, loud grinding and an age over ten years are signs that replacement is the better choice.
