    Picking a Tankless for Your Wyoming, MI Home

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Wyoming, MI

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Wyoming, MI

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Wyoming

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Wyoming

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Plan on four to six hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Wyoming, MI

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Wyoming, MI

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. We price both.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.
