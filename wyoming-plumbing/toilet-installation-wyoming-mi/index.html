    Types of Toilet Installation in Wyoming, MI

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Wyoming

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Wyoming

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Wyoming

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Wyoming

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Wyoming, MI

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Wyoming

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Wyoming, MI

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Wyoming

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.
