    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Wyoming

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet usually runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom.     A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Wyoming does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making major changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can advise you on local code requirements.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, typically takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, can take longer.
