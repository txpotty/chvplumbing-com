    Grease Trap Installation in Wyoming

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it.     Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Wyoming, MI

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space.     Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Wyoming, MI

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Wyoming, MI

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Wyoming

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Wyoming, MI

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Wyoming

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.
