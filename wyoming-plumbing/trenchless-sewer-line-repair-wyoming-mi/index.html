    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Wyoming

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Wyoming homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Sectional Point Repair in Wyoming

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Wyoming, MI

    Sewer lines in Wyoming, MI can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and foundation drainage are a big part of residential plumbing. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.
