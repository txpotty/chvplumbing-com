    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Wyoming, MI

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Wyoming

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Wyoming, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees.     Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Wyoming, MI

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.
