    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Wyoming

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Wyoming home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Wyoming

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Wyoming, MI

    Whether your home in Wyoming, MI uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the particular needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks ensure all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Wyoming, MI

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Wyoming, MI

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Wyoming

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We maintain 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Wyoming, MI

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Wyoming, MI

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Wyoming, MI, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.
