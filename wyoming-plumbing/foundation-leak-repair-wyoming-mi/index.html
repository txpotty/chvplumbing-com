    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Wyoming, MI

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. We dig a pit beside the foundation and hand-tunnel to the leak, which works well when the break sits under cabinets, a tiled shower or hardwood you do not want torn up. CHV Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Wyoming, MI repair is done.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Wyoming, MI

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Wyoming

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Wyoming, MI

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Look for wet insulation. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there. A sump pump may make sense in a Wyoming, MI crawl space that floods from outside.
