    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Wyoming, MI

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Wyoming, MI

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Wyoming

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Wyoming

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Wyoming, MI

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.
