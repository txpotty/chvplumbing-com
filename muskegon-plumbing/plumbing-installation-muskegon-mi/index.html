    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Muskegon

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. Each one is tested right after installation by a certified backflow tester, and the report goes to the water purveyor. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Muskegon

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. The drain line gets a proper air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Muskegon, MI

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Muskegon, MI

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and damp basements, which can mask slow leaks. Every unit gets isolation valves so it can be flushed.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Muskegon, MI

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. The expansion tank takes up that swing so the relief valve stops weeping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. For basement baths and guest suites below the sewer, we also install ejector systems with a sealed basin, check valve and vent.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Muskegon

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Muskegon, MI

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. Each branch is sized from the total BTU load and the run length so the last appliance on the line is not starved. New lines get pressure tested, always. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.
