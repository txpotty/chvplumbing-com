    Why Polybutylene Fails in Muskegon

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
