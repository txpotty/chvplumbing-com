    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Muskegon

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, CHV Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, allowing accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Muskegon, MI

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and musty basements, which can mask slow leaks.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Muskegon, MI

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Muskegon

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain nonstop, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Water Leak Detection for Insurance Claims in Muskegon

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Muskegon, MI

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Muskegon, MI

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.
