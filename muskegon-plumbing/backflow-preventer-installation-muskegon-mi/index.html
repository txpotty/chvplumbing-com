    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Muskegon

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Muskegon. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CHV Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Muskegon, MI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Muskegon, MI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Muskegon

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Muskegon, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.
