    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Muskegon

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Muskegon can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Muskegon

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, reducing disruption to your yard in Muskegon. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Muskegon, MI

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Muskegon

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and ends the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Muskegon.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Muskegon, MI

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Muskegon

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Muskegon, MI

    For individual pipe leaks, PEX can be used to repair or replace sections of damaged piping. Our plumbers can isolate the compromised section and tie in PEX, providing a durable and flexible fix that holds up to future pressures. This targeted approach is cost-effective for localized issues and brings the benefits of PEX to troubled areas of your existing plumbing in Muskegon, MI.
