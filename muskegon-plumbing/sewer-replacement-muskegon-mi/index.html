    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, which can lead to uneven settling of the slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and compact the backfill in lifts. Slow work near the house.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Muskegon, MI

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Muskegon

    Brittle pipe bursts cleanly. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.
