    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Muskegon, MI

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. CHV Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Muskegon

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Muskegon, MI

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Muskegon, MI soil.
