    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Muskegon, MI

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.
