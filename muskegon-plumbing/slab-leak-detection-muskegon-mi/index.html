    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Muskegon

    Slab leak detection in Muskegon usually costs $150 to $450. Repairs range from about $800 for a simple reroute to $3,000 or more for direct access under finished floors. We provide a written estimate after locating the leak.

    Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with fixtures off, a moving water meter and an unexplained jump in your water bill are common signs. Cracked tile, damp carpet and mildew odors also point to a slab leak. Call us if you notice any of these.

    Common signs of a slab leak include surprisingly high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also indicate moisture buildup.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes susceptible to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    For significant slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required. CHV Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes so your repair is compliant with local codes and regulations, protecting your home.

    Many policies cover the cost to access the leak and repair resulting water damage, though the pipe itself may not be covered. Coverage varies by insurer. We provide photos and a detailed report for your claim.
