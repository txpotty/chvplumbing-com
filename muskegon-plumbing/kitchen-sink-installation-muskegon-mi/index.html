    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Muskegon

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Muskegon

    Drop-in sinks are common in Michigan homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Muskegon

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first.     Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Muskegon

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Muskegon

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.
