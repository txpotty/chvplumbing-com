    Sump Pump Types We Install in Muskegon, MI

    Pedestal Pump Service in Muskegon, MI

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Muskegon

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Muskegon

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Muskegon, MI

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Muskegon

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Muskegon, MI

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.
