    Walk-In Shower Installation in Muskegon

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Muskegon, MI

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Muskegon, MI

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower.
