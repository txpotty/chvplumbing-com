    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening.     Clack Water Softener Replacement in South Lyon

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Metered Softener Replacement in South Lyon

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.
