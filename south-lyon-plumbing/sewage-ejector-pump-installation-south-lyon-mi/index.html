    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in South Lyon

    An ejector failure often starts with the high water alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit.     Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in South Lyon

    Residential ejector systems in South Lyon serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in South Lyon

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. The float usually goes first.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in South Lyon, MI

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. We price both options.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in South Lyon

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in South Lyon

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.
