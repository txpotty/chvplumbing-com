    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Bowl Replacement in South Lyon

    Toilet bowl replacement typically costs $250 to $550, including the matched bowl, new seals, hardware and labor. Specialty or discontinued colors can increase parts cost. We compare that against full replacement so you know the best option.

    We install and replace all major toilet bowl brands, including American Standard, Kohler, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and more. We can help you select a durable and efficient model that fits your needs.

    In many cases, if only the bowl is damaged, such as with a crack, we can replace just the toilet bowl itself, assuming a compatible tank is available and in good condition.     While it might seem simple, toilet bowl replacement involves proper sealing, flange inspection, and a watertight connection to prevent leaks and costly water damage. We recommend professional installation to make sure it's done correctly and to code.

    A wax ring is a crucial seal located at the base of your toilet bowl, creating a watertight barrier between the toilet and the drain pipe. It prevents water from leaking onto your floor every time you flush. When replacing a toilet bowl, installing a new wax ring is a must for a leak-free and odor-free installation.

    Small cosmetic chips above the waterline can be patched, but cracks below the waterline or near bolt holes cannot be reliably repaired. Replacement is the safe choice.
