    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in South Lyon

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in South Lyon

    The cost of gas water heater repair in South Lyon, MI can vary a lot depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosis.

    Generally, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. If the repair involves significant changes to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the South Lyon building department might be necessary. We handle all required permitting.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, inspecting the anode rod and checking the pressure relief valve, can significantly extend the life of your gas water heater and prevent common issues. Consider a maintenance plan with CHV Plumbing.

    Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the matching part.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    We repair Rheem, Ruud, A.O. Smith, State, Bradford White, American Standard and Whirlpool gas tanks, among others. We carry common pilot and valve parts.

    Gas tank water heaters typically last 8 to 12 years. Hard water sediment and skipped maintenance can cut that.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.
