    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in South Lyon

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. CHV Plumbing repairs both, across all the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.

    Slow Drain Repair in South Lyon

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section.
