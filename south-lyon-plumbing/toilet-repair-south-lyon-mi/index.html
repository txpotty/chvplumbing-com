    Running Toilet Fixes in South Lyon

    A toilet that continually runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in South Lyon is typically caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with high-quality, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop unnecessary water consumption.

    Fill Valve Replacement in South Lyon

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in South Lyon, MI

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. While some can be cleared with a plunger, deeper blockages may indicate issues further down the drain line or even in the main sewer line. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to pinpoint and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, making sure your toilet drains freely again.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in South Lyon, MI

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.
