    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in South Lyon

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in South Lyon, MI

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in South Lyon

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in South Lyon

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Some jobs need added vent or gas work.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in South Lyon, MI

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in South Lyon

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough.
