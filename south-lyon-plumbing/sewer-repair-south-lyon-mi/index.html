    Sewer Line Repair Jobs We Handle in South Lyon, MI

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in South Lyon

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in South Lyon

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in South Lyon

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. Night and weekend work is available.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in South Lyon, MI

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in South Lyon

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in South Lyon, MI

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in South Lyon we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in South Lyon

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in South Lyon often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in South Lyon

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.
