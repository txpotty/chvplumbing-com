    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in South Lyon, MI

    Copper Service Line Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in South Lyon, MI

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in South Lyon

    We start at the meter. With every tap closed, the leak indicator should sit still. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, rule out a running toilet or water softener, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.

    PEX Water Line Installation in South Lyon, MI

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Draining outdoor spigots in the fall and keeping heat on in vacant homes stop many of the burst pipes plumbers see each winter. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in South Lyon

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in South Lyon, MI

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.
