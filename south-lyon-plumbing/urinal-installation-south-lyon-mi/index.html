    Sensor Urinal Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in South Lyon

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in South Lyon, MI

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in South Lyon, MI

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in South Lyon, MI

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Each Sloan Royal gets the right inside parts for the bowl, a tuned control stop and a rebuild kit on hand. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in South Lyon, MI

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in South Lyon
