    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in South Lyon, MI

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in South Lyon, MI

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in South Lyon

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
