    Why Choose CHV Plumbing for Toilet Installation in South Lyon, MI?

    Most leaking toilets we get called back to were installed by someone else with the flange too low or the bowl never shimmed. The usual shortcut is two wax rings stacked up. It fails. We size the fix to the real gap with an extender ring or a thick foam seal.

    • Right Tools, A level, composite shims and a light hand on the bolts keep the porcelain intact.
    • Water Efficiency, A 1.28 gallon toilet uses less than half the water of an old 3.5 gallon model.
    • Code Compliance, Each install meets South Lyon and Michigan plumbing code for side clearance and venting, with the drain checked for slope.

    Citywide Coverage

    We install toilets in every part of South Lyon, from older neighborhoods with cast iron drains to new subdivisions plumbed in PVC. Each needs different parts. Older homes often need a flange repair ring or a new closet bend, while newer homes usually need nothing more than a seal and bolts.

    Experienced Team

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in South Lyon

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in South Lyon, MI

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in South Lyon

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks.     Commercial Toilet Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in South Lyon

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in South Lyon

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in South Lyon, MI

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.
