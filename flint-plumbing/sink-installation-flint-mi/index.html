    Kitchen Sink Installation in Flint

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Flint, MI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Flint

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Flint

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Flint, MI

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. That does not work on undermounts. Those get plumbed from below.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Flint, MI

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Flint

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.
