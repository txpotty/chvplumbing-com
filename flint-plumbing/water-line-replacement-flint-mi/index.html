    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Flint

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Flint, MI

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Flint, MI

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Flint

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

Sod goes back on top.
