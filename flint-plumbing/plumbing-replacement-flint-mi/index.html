    Water Softener Replacement in Flint, MI

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Flint home.

    Gas Line Replacement in Flint

    Old or damaged gas lines pose a serious safety risk and need immediate attention.     Sewer Line Replacement in Flint

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. We offer complete sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, limiting disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with careful attention. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local Flint plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Homes in Flint, MI with older galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes often experience low water pressure, discolored water or frequent leaks. A whole-home repipe with new PEX or copper piping can greatly improve water quality and pressure and eliminate leak concerns. We carefully plan and complete repiping projects, minimizing disruption and making sure all new pipes are properly routed, insulated and connected, providing a durable and efficient plumbing system for decades to come.
