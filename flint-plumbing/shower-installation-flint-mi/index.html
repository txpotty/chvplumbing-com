    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Flint, MI

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Most installs finish in a day.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Flint, MI

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Inspection comes next. We return for trim after tile.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Flint, MI

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. The valve is where to spend. The quote covers everything up front.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Flint

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Flint, MI

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Flint

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Digital Shower Systems in Flint, MI

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Flint

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. Then the valve. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.
