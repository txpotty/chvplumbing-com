    Foundation, Shower and Block Wall Waterproofing in Flint, MI

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Flint, MI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Flint

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Flint

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Flint take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Flint

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Flint, MI

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Flint

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Drainage System Integration in Flint, MI

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.
