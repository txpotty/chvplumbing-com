    Bathroom Drain Repair in Flint

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Flint

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. The footage tells us what to fix.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Flint, MI

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Flint

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and replace the damaged pipe so roof water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. We check the grade at the outlet too.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Flint

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, and where a vent was never installed we can add an air admittance valve in locations where local code permits it. Proper venting protects every trap in the house.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Flint, MI

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Poor original work causes some. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Flint, MI

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Repairs meet local plumbing code.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Flint

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, and when grease has built up deep in the branch, we jet it clean and check the slope so it does not come right back. We look at the disposal outlet too.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, and poor original work like unprimed glue joints or missing support straps all cause early failure. It is usually a combination.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge catches debris over and over, even after the line is cleared. A camera shows which one you have.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Pitch is the big one. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the line stays clear with normal use and a little care about grease. Older lines may still need yearly cleaning.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, so a drain that clogs repeatedly usually needs repair rather than more snaking. A camera tells you which.

    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. It is the clearest sign.     Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. So is water under the sink. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or a stain on the ceiling below a tub or shower, means the pipe or a fitting needs repair rather than another cleaning. Catch it early.
