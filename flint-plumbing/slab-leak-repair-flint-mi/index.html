    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Flint

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Flint.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Flint, MI

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Flint

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Flint home is no exception.
