    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Flint

    The cost of water heater installation in Flint, MI can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. CHV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to confirm the work meets local building and safety codes. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and sewer or water line work generally require a permit and inspection. CHV Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Flint as part of our service.
