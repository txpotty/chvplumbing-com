    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Flint, MI

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Flint

    Water flooding a hallway or seeping through grout needs fast attention. Shut the main. We respond quickly, isolate the leak and usually get water back to the rest of the home that day, then come back to locate and repair the line properly.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Flint, MI

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Flint, MI

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Flint home.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Flint

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.
