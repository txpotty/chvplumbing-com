    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Flint

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Flint, MI

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Flint, MI

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.
