    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Flint

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Flint.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Flint, MI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.
