    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Flint

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Flint, MI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Flint, MI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Flint

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Flint. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CHV Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.
