    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Flint

    The cost of hydro jetting in Flint, MI typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access.     For most homes in Flint, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can suggest a schedule based on your needs.

    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often misses.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or collapsed.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why CHV Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to assess your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is significantly more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively.
