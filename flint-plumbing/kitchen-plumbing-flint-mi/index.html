    What Our Kitchen Plumbers Fix and Install in Flint, MI

    New Disposal Installation in Flint

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. The trap often has to drop a couple of inches because the disposal outlet sits lower than the sink tailpiece did, and the wall stub has to allow it.

    InSinkErator Installation in Flint, MI

    Trading an older InSinkErator for an Evolution model is a common request because the newer units are quieter and grind finer. Often the mount stays. When the old Quick Lock mount is sound, we can reuse it, which saves time and leaves the sink flange alone, and we register the warranty for you.

    InSinkErator Models We Service in Flint

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which you notice in an open floor plan, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. We stock the common sizes. We also service older InSinkErator units when the motor is still sound.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Flint, MI

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    Grease Trap Installation in Flint, MI

    Under-sink grease traps work for small kitchens, while full restaurants often need an in-ground interceptor sized to the local sewer authority's rules. We read the requirement before we quote. Sizing is based on fixture flow, and an undersized trap fills fast and lets grease pass straight into the sewer line.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Flint, MI

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. Sometimes the pipe itself is the problem. A sag in the branch line or a drain arm with too little slope will hold grease no matter how often it gets cleaned.

    Grease Trap Cleaning in Flint

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again and odor stays inside. We log every visit. Inspectors usually want to see that log before anything else.

    Burst Supply Lines in Flint, MI

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. We replace both sides. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.
