    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Flint, MI

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Installed tanks often run well over a thousand dollars, and regular flushing and anode replacement help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan, while tankless units can last many years with proper descaling. Old tanks may be done. We give you real numbers for both.

    Anode Rod Checks in Flint

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Flint

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Flint

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. We test the gas connection too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Flint, MI

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Electric Element Service in Flint, MI

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Flint, MI

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.
