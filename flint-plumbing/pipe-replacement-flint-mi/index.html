    PEX Pipe Installation in Flint

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Flint due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. CHV Plumbing carefully installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Flint

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a serious concern for homeowners in Flint. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. CHV Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak. Depending on the severity and accessibility, we offer targeted pipe repair or rerouting and replacement solutions to permanently resolve the issue, protecting your home's structural integrity.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Flint, MI

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Flint

    If your home in Flint is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most sensible long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping. This upgrades your entire water delivery system, greatly improving water quality and pressure while eliminating future leak concerns from aging infrastructure.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Flint, MI

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Flint

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and unheated additions are the most common places for a freeze to cause a break. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. CHV Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Replacement in Flint

    A whole-home PEX repipe for a typical single-story home usually costs $5,000 to $12,000, while copper repipes and larger homes can run $10,000 to $18,000. Replacing a single section or branch line often costs $500 to $3,000. We give a written price after inspection.

    Small accessible repairs may be possible for experienced DIYers. Whole-home repiping requires proper tools, code knowledge, permits and inspection.

    Most cities require a permit for a whole-home repipe. We obtain the permit and schedule inspections as part of the job.

    Modern PEX pipes typically last 25 to 50 years, and copper pipes can last 50 to 70 years or even longer, depending on water quality and installation. This is a significant improvement over older materials like galvanized steel, which often fail within 20 to 50 years.

    Most repipes take two to four days, depending on home size and access. We restore water each evening so you can stay at the home.

    We mainly use high-quality PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for replacements. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and good for freeze protection, while copper is highly durable, long-lasting, and offers great water quality. We will discuss the best option for your home's specific needs and budget.

    PEX is more flexible, resists scale and does not develop pinhole leaks. Copper is rigid, heat tolerant and favored by some homeowners, so we price both.
