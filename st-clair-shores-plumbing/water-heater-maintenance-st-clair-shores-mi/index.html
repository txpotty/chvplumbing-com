    Tank Water Heater Flushing in St. Clair Shores

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in St. Clair Shores

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in St. Clair Shores, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in St. Clair Shores

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
