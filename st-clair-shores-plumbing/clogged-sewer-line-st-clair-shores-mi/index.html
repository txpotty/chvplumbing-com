    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in St. Clair Shores, MI

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. You see the footage first.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in St. Clair Shores, MI

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.
