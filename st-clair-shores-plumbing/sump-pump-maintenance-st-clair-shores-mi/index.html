    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod.     Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in St. Clair Shores

    Watch for a pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water, cycles every minute or two, or grinds. A stuck float, rust on the housing or a musty smell near the pit also point to trouble.
