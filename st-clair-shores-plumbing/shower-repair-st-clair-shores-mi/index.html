    Shower Diverter Repair in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Shower Repair in St. Clair Shores, MI

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. CHV Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in St. Clair Shores

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Valve Replacement in St. Clair Shores

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. New valves include pressure balancing. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in St. Clair Shores

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.
