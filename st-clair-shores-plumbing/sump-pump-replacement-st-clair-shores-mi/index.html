    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in St. Clair Shores, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in St. Clair Shores

    Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Backup Pump Options in St. Clair Shores, MI
