    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in St. Clair Shores

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.
