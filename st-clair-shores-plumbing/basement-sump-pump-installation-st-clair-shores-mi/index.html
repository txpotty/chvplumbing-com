    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in St. Clair Shores

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation.     Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain.     A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, designed to handle everyday water removal.
