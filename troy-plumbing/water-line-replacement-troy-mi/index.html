    Main Water Line Replacement in Troy

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. After a second leak in a new spot, we stop recommending patches. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Troy, MI

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Troy

    Sometimes the open trench wins. If the old pipe has collapsed or turns hard corners, we dig. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, set the pipe on clean bedding, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Troy

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Troy, MI

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. Shutdowns get scheduled for when you are closed. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Troy

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will close the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Troy, MI

Better pressure upstairs.
