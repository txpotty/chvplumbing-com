    Types of Toilet Replacements in Troy, MI

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Troy

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Troy, MI

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Current WaterSense models are rated at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Troy, MI

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Troy, MI

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. Multiple toilets can be swapped in a single trip. Tenants keep a working bathroom. We coordinate entry directly with them, so you do not have to be there.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Troy, MI

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Troy, MI

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Troy, MI

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Troy, MI

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
