    Where We Find Leaks in Troy, MI

    Water Line Leak Detection in Troy, MI

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. Your water bill is usually the first warning. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Troy home.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Troy

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Troy

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Troy, MI

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Troy, MI

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Troy, MI

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Gas Leak Detection in Troy

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Get out first if it is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, with repairs made to the fuel gas code before the gas comes back on.

    Slab Leak Signs in Troy, MI

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, then lay out the spot repair, reroute and repipe choices with a price on each.
