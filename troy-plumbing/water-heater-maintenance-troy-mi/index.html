    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Troy

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Troy

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Troy, MI

    Small drips become floods. Connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the seams get checked, along with the pan and floor. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Troy

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Troy

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.
