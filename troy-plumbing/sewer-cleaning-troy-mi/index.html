    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Troy, MI

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Older neighborhoods may also have lead service lines, which the state is working to replace over time. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Troy

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Troy, MI

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will often show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the smart move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Troy

This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. CHV Plumbing employs professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Troy, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes virtually spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.
