    Relief Valve Testing in Troy

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup.     Standard Tank Flushing in Troy

    Electric Element Check in Troy
