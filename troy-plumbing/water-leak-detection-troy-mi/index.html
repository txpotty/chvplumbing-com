    Slab Leak Detection in Troy

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. CHV Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Troy, MI

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Troy

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. CHV Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Troy

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.
