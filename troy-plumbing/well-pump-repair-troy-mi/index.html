    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Troy

    Jet Pump System Repair in Troy, MI

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CHV Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Troy operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Troy

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Troy.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Troy

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.
