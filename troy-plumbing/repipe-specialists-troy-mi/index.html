    Reliable Repipe Specialists Services in Troy, MI

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Troy

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Copper Repiping in Troy, MI

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Troy, MI

    Many older homes in Troy, MI still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing problems but also significantly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Drain Line Repiping in Troy

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as crucial as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. CHV Plumbing provides comprehensive drain line repiping in Troy, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Emergency Repipe Services in Troy, MI

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Troy

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Troy, MI

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Troy, MI

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Troy, MI has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage.
