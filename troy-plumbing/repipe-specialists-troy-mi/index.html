    Type L Copper Repiping in Troy

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Troy, MI

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Troy, MI

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Troy

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Troy

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed.
