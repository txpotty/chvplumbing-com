    Softener Systems We Replace in Troy, MI

    Metered Softener Replacement in Troy

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Troy, MI

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Troy

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Troy

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Troy, MI

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. You see the choices on one page.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Troy

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Troy, MI

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Troy

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.
