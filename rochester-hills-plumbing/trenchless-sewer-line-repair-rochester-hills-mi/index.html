    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Rochester Hills

    Before any trenchless repair, we perform a thorough video inspection of your sewer line.     Sectional Point Repair in Rochester Hills

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Rochester Hills

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    For businesses in Rochester Hills, MI, minimizing disruption is key. Our commercial trenchless sewer line repair services offer a low-profile and efficient way to address sewer issues without impacting business operations or customer access. Whether it is a restaurant, office building or retail space, we provide durable trenchless solutions that restore sewer function quickly, so your business can keep operating smoothly.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Rochester Hills, MI

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Michigan homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Rochester Hills

This allows us to accurately diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and lasting repair in Rochester Hills.
