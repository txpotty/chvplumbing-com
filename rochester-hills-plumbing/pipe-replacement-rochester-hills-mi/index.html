    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Rochester Hills

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Many older homes in Rochester Hills, MI still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion restricts water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.

    PEX Repiping in Rochester Hills

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Rochester Hills

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be destructive and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. CHV Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.
