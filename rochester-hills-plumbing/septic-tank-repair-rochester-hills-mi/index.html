    Common causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria.     To prevent future septic tank problems, limit water usage, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, use septic-safe cleaning products, never pour grease down drains and schedule regular septic tank pumping every 3 to 5 years. Regular inspections can also catch issues early.

    Key signs include slow drains, gurgling toilets, sewage odors inside or outside your home, unusually lush or soggy spots in your yard near the septic tank or drain field, and sewage backups into your home.
