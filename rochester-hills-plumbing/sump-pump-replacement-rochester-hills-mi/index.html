    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Rochester Hills, MI

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Look at the breaker and the GFCI outlet, then pull the lid and check the float for a hang-up. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. We stock the common pumps on the truck, and most emergency swaps are done on the first trip.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Backup Pump Options in Rochester Hills, MI

    Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. The backup kicks on if the primary dies or the power goes out. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. They will not work on a well.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Rochester Hills, MI

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Rochester Hills

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.
