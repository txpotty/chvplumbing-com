    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Rochester Hills

    CHV Plumbing offers a thorough tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is essential wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Rochester Hills

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Rochester Hills home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Rochester Hills, MI

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Rochester Hills

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Rochester Hills

    If you notice sudden changes in your hot water tank's performance, like strange noises, discolored water or a sudden drop in hot water supply, CHV Plumbing offers emergency inspections in Rochester Hills. While not a full maintenance, we can promptly diagnose the issue and advise on immediate steps. That usually includes a focused maintenance procedure to restore function and prevent further damage.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.
