    PEX Water Line Installation in Rochester Hills, MI

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and poorly insulated additions are the most common places for a freeze to cause a break.     PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Rochester Hills

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.
