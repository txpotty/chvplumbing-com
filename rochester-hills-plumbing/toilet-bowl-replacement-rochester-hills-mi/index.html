    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Rochester Hills, MI

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off.
