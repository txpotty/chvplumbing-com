    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Rochester Hills, MI

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Rochester Hills

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Rochester Hills

    Some homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces techs to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent. We can install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so the line can be cleaned from either direction. This makes every future cleaning quicker and cheaper. It also gives a clear access point for camera inspections. Cleanout installation is done to local code with any needed permit.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Rochester Hills

    The main sewer line is essential for carrying wastewater away from your home. Blockages in this line can cause whole-house backups affecting all fixtures. We use powerful augers and specialized cutting tools to clear roots, grease, and other debris that commonly obstruct main sewer lines, especially in older homes around Rochester Hills.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Rochester Hills

    Proactive maintenance is key to avoiding costly sewer emergencies. CHV Plumbing offers preventative sewer cleaning in Rochester Hills designed to keep your main line clear before problems arise.     Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Rochester Hills, MI

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. Homes around Rochester Hills on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.
