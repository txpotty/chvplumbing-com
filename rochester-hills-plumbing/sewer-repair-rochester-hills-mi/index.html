    Root Intrusion Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Rochester Hills, MI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Rochester Hills, MI

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Rochester Hills

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Rochester Hills

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole.
