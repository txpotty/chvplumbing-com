    Walk-In Shower Installation in Rochester Hills

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Rochester Hills, MI

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Handles and the head come later. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Rochester Hills

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area.
