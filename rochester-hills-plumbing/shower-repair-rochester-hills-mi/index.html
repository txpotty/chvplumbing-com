    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Rochester Hills

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not.     Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Rochester Hills, MI

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Rochester Hills

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. We cut it out with an auger. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Rochester Hills

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Rochester Hills

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.
