    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Rochester Hills

    The cost of new construction plumbing in Rochester Hills varies a lot based on the size and complexity of the project, number of bathrooms, and fixture choices. For a standard 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home, rough-in and finish plumbing can range from $10,000 to $25,000. Commercial projects or luxury homes with advanced systems run higher.

    CHV Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on all our new construction plumbing installations. This covers both materials and labor, ensuring peace of mind that your new plumbing system in Rochester Hills is protected against defects for a specified period after completion.

    Several inspections are needed during new construction plumbing. These typically include an underground or rough-in inspection before walls are closed, a top-out inspection if applicable, and a final inspection after all fixtures are installed and the system is operational. We schedule and manage all inspections to ensure compliance.

    We can run a dedicated return line during rough-in so hot water reaches distant bathrooms in seconds. A dedicated line works better than retrofit crossover valves and adds roughly $800 to $2,000 depending on layout. It also saves the water that would otherwise run down the drain as you wait for hot water.

    The plumbing rough-in phase for a typical residential build in Rochester Hills usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, depending on the crew size and complexity. The final fixture installation and trim-out usually takes an additional 2 to 4 days later in the construction schedule.

    Slow drains on a brand new home, gurgling fixtures, sewer odors or a stub-out that ended up under a wall are typical red flags. These usually trace back to poor slope, missing vents or layout errors before the pour. A camera inspection can pinpoint the cause before the problem grows.

    Our workmanship is warrantied, and the warranty terms are listed in your contract. Fixtures, water heaters and PEX systems also carry their own manufacturer warranties, often 10 years or more for PEX and fittings from brands like Uponor. We hand over the paperwork at final.
