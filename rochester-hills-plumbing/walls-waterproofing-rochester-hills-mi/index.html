    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Rochester Hills, MI

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Rochester Hills

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals.
