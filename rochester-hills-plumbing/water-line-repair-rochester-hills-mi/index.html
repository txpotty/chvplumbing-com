    Water Line Repair We Handle in Rochester Hills, MI

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Rochester Hills

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Rochester Hills

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Homes around Rochester Hills on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Main Water Line Repair in Rochester Hills

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Rochester Hills

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Rochester Hills

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Rochester Hills

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes typically use copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.
