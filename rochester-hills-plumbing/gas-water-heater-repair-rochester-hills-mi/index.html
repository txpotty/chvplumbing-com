    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Rochester Hills

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Rochester Hills, MI

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Rochester Hills

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system.     Venting and Draft Repair in Rochester Hills, MI
