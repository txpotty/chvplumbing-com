    Damaged Baffle Repair in Battle Creek

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Battle Creek, MI

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Battle Creek

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. It sets up like candle wax on the crown of the line. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. Jetting removes it.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Battle Creek, MI

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. Clearing the pipe without fixing the trap just resets the clock.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Battle Creek

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. Undersized traps fail in predictable spots. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We repair what broke, then tell you straight whether the unit is sized for your volume.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Battle Creek

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. A bellied pipe will keep clogging no matter how often you jet it.
