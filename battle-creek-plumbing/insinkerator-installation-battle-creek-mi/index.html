    Hard Water and Disposals in Battle Creek

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Battle Creek, MI

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Battle Creek

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. That seal cannot be fixed. We disconnect the old unit, cap the dishwasher hose while we work and set the new disposal on the same drain geometry, adjusting the tailpiece if the new one sits at a different height.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Battle Creek, MI

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Evolution Series Installation in Battle Creek

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Badger Series Installation in Battle Creek, MI

That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.
