    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Battle Creek, MI

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless.     Typically, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage.     Swapping a pump in an existing pit typically takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching typically takes most of a day.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing.
