    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Battle Creek, MI

    Original sewer pipe in older homes is usually clay, cast iron or Orangeburg. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, slowly flattens until almost nothing passes. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Battle Creek

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench.     Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Battle Creek

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks.     Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp.
