    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Battle Creek

    Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains.     Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Battle Creek

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Battle Creek, MI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Water heaters run harder through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Battle Creek, MI

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.
