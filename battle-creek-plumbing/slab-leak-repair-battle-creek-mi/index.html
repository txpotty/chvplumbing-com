    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Battle Creek

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Battle Creek with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
