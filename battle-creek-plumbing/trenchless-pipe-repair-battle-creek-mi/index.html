    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Battle Creek

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one.     Commercial sites in Battle Creek have longer runs and more laterals. Parking lots sit on top. Schools, offices and stores keep lots and walkways open because we work from cleanouts and manholes, cutting only small pits. We schedule around your hours.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Battle Creek

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Battle Creek yard.
