    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Battle Creek, MI

    Vessel Sink Installation in Battle Creek, MI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Battle Creek, MI

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Battle Creek, MI

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Battle Creek

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Battle Creek, MI

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Battle Creek

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Battle Creek, MI

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Battle Creek

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.
