    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Livonia

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Livonia

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. We replace it, along with corroded galvanized and pinholed copper, using PEX or copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. Drywall patches are included.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Livonia

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues. Nothing gets quoted until the leak is pinpointed. Repeat leaks usually point to a repipe instead of another patch.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Livonia, MI

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Older neighborhoods may also have lead service lines, which the state is working to replace over time. Repipes are done in PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Backups in Livonia

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. We haul the old tank away.
