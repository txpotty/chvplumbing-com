    Water lines can leak or break due to several factors, including aging pipes (corrosion in galvanized steel or copper), shifting soil, tree root intrusion, high water pressure, ground freezing and thawing cycles, and accidental damage during excavation.     We typically install Type K copper, PEX rated for direct burial or HDPE for trenchless pulls. Each holds up well in most soils when properly bedded. We will recommend the right material based on your soil, line length and price range.

    Common signs of a water line leak include an unexplained increase in your water bill, damp or soggy spots in your yard (even during dry weather), a clear drop in water pressure, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.
