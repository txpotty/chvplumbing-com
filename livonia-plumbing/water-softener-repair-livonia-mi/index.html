    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Livonia, MI

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Livonia, MI

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Resin Bed Problems in Livonia

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Livonia, MI

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Livonia

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. CHV Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Livonia, MI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Livonia, MI

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. CHV Plumbing repairs these units in Livonia, MI. The media wears out in a few years.

    Control Valve Repair in Livonia

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. CHV Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Parts for the common valves are stocked, which keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Livonia, MI

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.
