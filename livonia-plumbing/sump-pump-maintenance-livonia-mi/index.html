    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Livonia

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails.     Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Livonia

    Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Livonia, MI

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.
