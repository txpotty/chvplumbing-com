    Battery Backup System Testing in Livonia, MI

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Float Switch Checks in Livonia, MI

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Livonia, MI

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. With regular service, seven to ten years is typical. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Livonia

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Livonia

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Livonia

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Livonia

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Livonia

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.
