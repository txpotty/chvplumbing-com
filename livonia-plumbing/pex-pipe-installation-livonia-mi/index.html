    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Livonia

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Livonia

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. A switch to PEX ends it. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Livonia

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Livonia, MI

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Livonia, MI come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Livonia, MI

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Livonia, MI

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Livonia, MI

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. Additions get their own sizing. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. Cold weather slows it.
