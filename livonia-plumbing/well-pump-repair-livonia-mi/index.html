    Pressure Switch Replacement in Livonia

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Careful diagnosis is the first step to an effective well pump repair.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Livonia, MI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay.     Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear.     Well Pressure Tank Repair in Livonia

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation usually point to a faulty pressure tank.     Jet Pump System Repair in Livonia, MI

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CHV Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Livonia operates at peak efficiency.
