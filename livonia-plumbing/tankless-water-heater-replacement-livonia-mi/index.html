    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Livonia, MI

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Livonia, MI

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We set a schedule. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Livonia

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Livonia
