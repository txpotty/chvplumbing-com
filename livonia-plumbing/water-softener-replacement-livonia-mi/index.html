    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Livonia

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Livonia, MI

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Livonia

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Livonia

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Livonia, MI

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. We show both options side by side.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Livonia

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Nearby Water Softener Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Most softener jobs around Livonia are finished in an afternoon. We carry common 32,000 and 48,000-grain systems, so a failed unit can often be swapped the same day you call. Larger units may need a day.
