    Copper Pipe Replacement in Livonia

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Livonia

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Livonia

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Livonia

    Most poly replacements in Livonia fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price. Small homes with one bath and an open attic cost the least, and big multi-bath houses or copper jobs cost the most. The quote comes in writing at the visit.

    Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Big houses need more time. The schedule depends on the number of fixtures, attic access and whether you choose PEX or copper, and water is usually back on at the end of each work day.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. Uponor is our usual pick. For copper, we install Type L tubing from established mills rather than the thinner Type M found in many older homes, because the thicker wall holds up better over time.

    Most homes take two to four days for plumbing, plus a day for drywall patching. Water is usually restored each evening.

    Both work well in Michigan homes. PEX bends, tolerates freezes and costs less. Copper is stiff, long lasting and fine in sun and heat, and we will help you choose based on the house, water and budget.

    Gray or blue plastic pipe stamped PB2110 is the main sign. Cracked fittings are a warning. Since poly can look normal right before it splits, a home from roughly 1978 to 1995 with this pipe should plan a replacement even before any leaks.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty on the repipe, and pipe makers offer their own long-term warranties. You get the details before we start.

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does. Scale builds inside. Scale buildup can reduce flow and add stress to pipe that is already brittle, so in hard water areas we often suggest adding a softener loop during the replacement.
