    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Livonia, MI

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Livonia, MI

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be frustrating for homeowners in Livonia, MI.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Livonia

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
