    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Livonia, MI

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Livonia

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Livonia

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Livonia

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.
