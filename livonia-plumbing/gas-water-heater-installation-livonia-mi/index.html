    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Livonia

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Then we test it. Value and reliability are why people choose them.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Livonia

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. We strap it if code calls for it. Sizing follows household demand.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Livonia, MI

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Livonia, MI

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. CHV Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Livonia

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. Plan on a full day. We do all of it to code.

    Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Livonia, MI

    An atmospheric vent tank is the cheapest gas install. Power vent and tankless units cost more, and vent changes, gas line upgrades and code corrections on older installs can add to any job. Permits and haul-away are included. We also compare gas and electric operating costs.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Livonia

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.
