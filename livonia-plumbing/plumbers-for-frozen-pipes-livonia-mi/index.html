    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Livonia

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and suggest draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can set that up.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Livonia, MI

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX connections. Leaks usually appear only after the ice melts, sometimes hours later. We shut off the water, open the wall or ceiling only where needed and replace the damaged section. Every repair is pressure tested so you know the line is solid.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Livonia
