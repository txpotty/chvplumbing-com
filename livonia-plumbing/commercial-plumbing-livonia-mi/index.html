    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Livonia, MI

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Livonia

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Livonia, MI

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Livonia, MI

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Livonia

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Livonia

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.
