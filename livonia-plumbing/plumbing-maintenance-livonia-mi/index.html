    Sump Pump Maintenance in Livonia, MI

    Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Livonia.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Livonia

    Newer homes typically use copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Livonia, MI

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your choices.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Livonia, MI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Gas Line and Repipe Inspections in Livonia, MI

    Safety is critical with gas lines, and our maintenance includes thorough inspections for leaks, corrosion, and proper valve operation. For older homes in Livonia with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipes, our repipe specialists can assess the need for replacement. Regular checks help identify potential hazards or signs of coming pipe failure, preventing dangerous situations and significant property damage.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Livonia

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Livonia

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
