    Common Sink Repairs in Livonia, MI

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Livonia

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Livonia

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Livonia, MI

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Livonia

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Livonia

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Livonia

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Livonia, MI

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Livonia

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
