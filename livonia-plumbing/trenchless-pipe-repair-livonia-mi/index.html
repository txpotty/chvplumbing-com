    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Livonia

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Livonia

    Sewer line issues can be a headache, but our trenchless sewer line repair services reduce the mess and disruption.     Pipe Bursting Services in Livonia, MI

    For badly damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Livonia, MI.
