    Sewer Lateral Lining in Livonia

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Livonia

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Small cracks get a sectional liner. Longer damage gets a full-length liner, and a fully collapsed line gets burst and replaced, all without trenching across the yard or cutting the driveway at your home.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Livonia, MI

    People are often surprised at the final number. Trenchless can cost less. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Emergency Trenchless Pipe Repair in Livonia, MI

    A collapsed sewer line stops every drain in the house. It can't wait. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life.     Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Livonia

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Livonia

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Concrete is costly to replace. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Livonia, MI

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Livonia

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.
