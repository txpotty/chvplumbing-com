    Rough-In and Top-Out Plumbing in Livonia, MI

    Water Heater and Recirculation Installation in Livonia, MI

    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Livonia

    Most new single-family homes in the Livonia area cost between $12,000 and $25,000 to plumb, with larger custom homes running higher. Each added bathroom adds several thousand dollars.     Plumbing is usually covered under the general building permit. If the jurisdiction wants a separate plumbing permit, our license goes on it. Either way, we schedule the plumbing inspections, attend them, and handle any corrections without the builder having to chase us down about it.

    PEX handles most water supply in new homes. It flexes and needs fewer fittings in the walls than copper. For drain, waste and vent we use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS, whichever your code and plans call for. We still use copper near water heaters.

    A local plumber knows which inspector wants the test gauge at the front door and which wants it at the manifold. That sounds minor.     Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and perimeter drains are a big part of residential plumbing. Soil movement works on any pipe buried under concrete. We bed drain lines in sand or gravel, sleeve every pipe that passes through concrete, and use flexible PEX for the water service where code allows it. Sleeves are cheap.
