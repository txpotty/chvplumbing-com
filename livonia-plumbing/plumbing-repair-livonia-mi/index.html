    Water Softener Repair in Livonia, MI

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures.     Well Pump Service in Livonia, MI

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. CHV Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. A pump drawing high amps is wearing out. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Livonia, MI

    Pinhole leaks in copper start from the inside, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Where there is one pinhole, there are often more nearby. We solder in a new section or use a press fitting, and we check the house pressure while we are there. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Slow Drain Repair in Livonia

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section. The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Sewer Line Repair in Livonia, MI

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up every few months. They find a cracked joint, grow inside and catch paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.
