    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections.
