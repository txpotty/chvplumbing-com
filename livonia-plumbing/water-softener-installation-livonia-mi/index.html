    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Livonia

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500, depending on how easy it is to reach the main line.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. A yearly checkup with a plumber catches worn seals, salt bridges in the brine tank and clogged injectors before they cause hard water. Keep the salt level up.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners alter the minerals instead, and softeners are stronger for very hard water.

    Homes with hard water usually benefit from one. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    A good softener usually lasts ten to fifteen years. Hardness, maintenance and build quality matter, and fresh resin can add years. Service helps.

    On an existing loop, installs usually take two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.
