    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Livonia

    Most water softener installations cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 depending on access.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for major plumbing alterations like water softener installation to confirm compliance with local codes. CHV Plumbing will advise you on any necessary permits for your Livonia installation and can assist with the application process.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more effective for very hard water.

    Installing on an existing loop usually takes two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.

    With proper maintenance, a quality water softener system can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. The lifespan depends on the quality of the unit, the hardness of your water and how well it is maintained. Regular servicing can help extend its life.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could indicate an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Many homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    Maintenance primarily involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, usually every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness. It's also good practice to schedule annual check-ups with a professional plumber to confirm the system is operating efficiently and to address any potential issues.
