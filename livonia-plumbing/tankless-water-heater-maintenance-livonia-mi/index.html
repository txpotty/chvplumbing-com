    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Livonia

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Livonia

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Livonia

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.
