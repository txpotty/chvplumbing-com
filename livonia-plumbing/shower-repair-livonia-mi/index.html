    Low Water Pressure in Shower Repair in Livonia, MI

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Livonia, MI

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints.     Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Livonia

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out.     Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Livonia, MI

We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Livonia

    Shower Diverter Repair in Livonia, MI

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Livonia
