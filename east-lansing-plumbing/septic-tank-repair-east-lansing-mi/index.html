    Drain Field Line Jetting in East Lansing

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Drain Field Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in East Lansing

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. Roots and settling ground cause most sags and breaks in that run.     Septic Lid and Riser Repair in East Lansing

    Effluent Pump Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.
