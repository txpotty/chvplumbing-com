    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Many older homes in East Lansing, MI still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. CHV Plumbing provides comprehensive galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also shields your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in East Lansing

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. CHV Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in East Lansing

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be destructive and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. CHV Plumbing uses advanced leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.

    PEX Pipe Installation in East Lansing

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in East Lansing due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Homes around East Lansing on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. CHV Plumbing carefully installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    PEX Repiping in East Lansing

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.
