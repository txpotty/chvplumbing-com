    PEX Pipe Installation in East Lansing

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Homes around East Lansing on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in East Lansing

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in East Lansing, MI

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.
