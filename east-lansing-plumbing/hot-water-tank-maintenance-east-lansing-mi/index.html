    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in East Lansing

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in East Lansing.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in East Lansing

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your East Lansing home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in East Lansing

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in East Lansing

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in East Lansing, MI

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is essential for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, ensuring it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in East Lansing, MI.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in East Lansing, MI

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an honest read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend.     Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in East Lansing, MI

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
