    Interior Walls Waterproofing in East Lansing, MI

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Basement Wall Sealing in East Lansing, MI

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and musty basements, which can mask slow leaks. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your East Lansing, MI basement.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in East Lansing, MI
