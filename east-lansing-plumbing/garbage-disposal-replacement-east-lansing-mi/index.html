    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in East Lansing

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in East Lansing, MI usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually enough. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your East Lansing home.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can usually be repaired.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can help.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it usually safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    Leaks from the body, frequent resets, constant jams, loud grinding and age over ten years are common signs.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from two to ten years, and we guarantee our labor.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.
