    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in East Lansing

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Bradford White and Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in East Lansing, MI

    We are proficient in repairing all major gas water heater brands, including popular models like Bradford White and Rheem, commonly found in East Lansing, MI homes. Our team understands the specific schematics and common failure points for these manufacturers. We stock or can promptly source genuine replacement parts, so your brand of gas water heater receives the expert attention it needs for a lasting repair.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in East Lansing, MI

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Pilot Light Repair in East Lansing

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Venting and Draft Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in East Lansing, MI

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your East Lansing, MI home.
