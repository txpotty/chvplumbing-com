    Types of Toilet Installation in East Lansing, MI

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in East Lansing, MI

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Standard Toilet Installation in East Lansing

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in East Lansing, MI

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in East Lansing

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in East Lansing

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Powered seats with warm water and a dryer need a GFCI outlet within a few feet, and if one is not there, we line up a licensed electrician before we mount anything. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in East Lansing

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in East Lansing

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in East Lansing

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Toilet Installation Near Me in East Lansing, MI

    Older East Lansing homes can hide surprises under the toilet, like a cast iron flange rusted thin or a subfloor gone soft from an old leak. We check for both before setting anything. Any soft plywood gets cut back and replaced with new, glued and screwed down. It adds an hour, sometimes two.

    Toilet installers near you in East Lansing should be licensed and insured, and they should be willing to pull a permit when the job calls for one. Ask about both on the phone. Moving a toilet or adding a new bathroom usually needs a permit and an inspection, while swapping a toilet in the same spot usually does not. We tell you which applies.
