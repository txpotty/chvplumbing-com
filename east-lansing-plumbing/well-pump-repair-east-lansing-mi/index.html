    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in East Lansing

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. CHV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a East Lansing, MI well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in East Lansing

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in East Lansing, MI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts.     Well Pump Diagnostic Services in East Lansing, MI

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Pressure Tank Repair in East Lansing, MI

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Jet Pump System Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. CHV Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.
