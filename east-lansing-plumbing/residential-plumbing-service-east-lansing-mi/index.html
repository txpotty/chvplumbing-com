    Water Heater Service and Installation in East Lansing, MI

    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. A tank heater starts leaking at the bottom or rumbling from sediment, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. Both types get flushed and repaired here.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in East Lansing

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. The usual clues are a warm patch of floor, a meter dial moving with everything off, or a sudden jump in the water bill. We pinpoint it before quoting. Fixes range from a direct repair to rerouting the line overhead or a full repipe when leaks keep coming back.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in East Lansing

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. We replace flappers, fill valves and wax rings, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. Rocking toilets get shimmed and caulked.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. Even a slow drip shows up on the water bill.

    Pipe Repair and Repipes in East Lansing, MI

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. Repeat leaks mean a repipe. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in East Lansing, MI

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Backups in East Lansing

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. One slow sink by itself is usually just the trap or a short branch line, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Roots are common in older clay lines.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in East Lansing, MI

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. Pipe size is figured from the BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.
