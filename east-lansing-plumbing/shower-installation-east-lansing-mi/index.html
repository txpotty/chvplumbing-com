    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in East Lansing

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Curbless Shower Installation in East Lansing

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in East Lansing

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in East Lansing, MI

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Custom Shower System Installation in East Lansing, MI

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in East Lansing, MI

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.
