    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in East Lansing

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.
