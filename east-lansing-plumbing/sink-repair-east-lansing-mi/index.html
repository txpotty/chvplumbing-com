    Kitchen Sink Repair in East Lansing

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in East Lansing, MI

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in East Lansing

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in East Lansing, MI

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in East Lansing

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in East Lansing, MI

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
