    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. A crusty fitting is the clue. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, and either resolder it properly or swap the fitting for a new one. Every joint gets tested.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Grand Rapids

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. Bills tell the story. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. We test before backfill.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Grand Rapids, MI

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, and a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over a few years than repeated repairs and drywall patches. We price both.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Grand Rapids

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Shutdowns cost money. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. We schedule around business hours.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Grand Rapids

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, and quarter-turn valves go in to replace the old multi-turn stops. We test each one.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Grand Rapids

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Services in Grand Rapids, MI

    In a commercial building, one copper leak can affect several tenants at once. Planning matters. We isolate the smallest zone possible and repair the leak with press fittings or solder, and we schedule the shutdown around business hours so customers and tenants are not affected. We document the repair.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Draining outdoor spigots in the fall and keeping heat on in vacant homes stop many of the burst pipes plumbers see each winter. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.
