    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Grand Rapids

    Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are some of the best defenses against flooded basements. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Grand Rapids

    CHV Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, ensuring the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Grand Rapids

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Grand Rapids

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Grand Rapids, MI

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Grand Rapids, MI.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Grand Rapids, MI

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, CHV Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Grand Rapids, MI. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Grand Rapids

    Over time, sump pits can collect dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, avoiding breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Grand Rapids property.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Grand Rapids, MI

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Grand Rapids, MI where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.
