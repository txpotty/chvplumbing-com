    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. CHV Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Grand Rapids, MI

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Grand Rapids.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Grand Rapids, MI

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Grand Rapids

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.
