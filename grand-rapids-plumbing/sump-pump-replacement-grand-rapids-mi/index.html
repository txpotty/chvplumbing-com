    Sump Pump Types We Install in Grand Rapids, MI

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Grand Rapids

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Grand Rapids, MI

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Grand Rapids

    Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Backup Pump Options in Grand Rapids, MI

    Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. The backup kicks on if the primary dies or the power goes out. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. Wells cannot run them.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Need more lift? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Grand Rapids

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.
