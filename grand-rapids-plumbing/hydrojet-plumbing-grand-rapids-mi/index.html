    Why Choose CHV Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Grand Rapids, MI?

    Hydrojetting cleans more thoroughly than any cable can. A cable leaves most of the buildup behind. Jetting removes grease, scale, sludge and soft roots from the full circumference of the pipe, restoring close to its original diameter, which is why a jetted line stays clear so much longer than a snaked one.

    • Prevents Recurrence, Removing the buildup means clogs return far less often.
    • Lasting Flow, A jetted line stays open much longer than one that was only cabled.
    • Verified Results, Before and after camera video shows what came out of the line.

    Citywide Coverage

    Homes with septic systems outside Grand Rapids get jetting too. Septic lines get careful passes. Jetting the line between the house and the tank clears grease and sludge without harming the tank's bacteria, since we use only water, and the camera can show whether the inlet baffle is intact.

    Experienced Team

    Years of jetting experience go into every job. Knowing when to stop matters. A line that will not clear after several passes may have a collapsed section, and pushing harder can bury the nozzle or damage the pipe, so our technicians pull back and camera it rather than forcing the issue.

    Safety-First Approach

    Our safety rules cover the crew and your property. Floors get covered first. When we jet from an indoor cleanout or fixture, we set up drop cloths and a splash guard, and we never jet a fixture line without confirming where it ties into the main so nothing backs up into another room.

    Root Removal Jetting in Grand Rapids

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them.     Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Grand Rapids, MI

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Grand Rapids

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Grand Rapids

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Grand Rapids
