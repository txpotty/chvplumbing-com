    PEX Pipe Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It handles a freeze better than rigid pipe. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and poorly insulated additions are the most common places for a freeze to cause a break.
