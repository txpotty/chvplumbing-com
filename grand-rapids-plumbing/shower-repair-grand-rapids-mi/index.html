    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Grand Rapids

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Grand Rapids, MI

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Grand Rapids

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
