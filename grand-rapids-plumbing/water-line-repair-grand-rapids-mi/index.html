    Main Water Line Repair in Grand Rapids

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, minimizing excavation and disruption to your property in Grand Rapids. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Grand Rapids

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Underground Leak Detection in Grand Rapids, MI

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them hard to detect without specialized equipment. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents costly damage to your foundation.
