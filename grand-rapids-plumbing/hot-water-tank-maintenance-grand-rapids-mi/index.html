    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Grand Rapids, MI

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be frustrating for homeowners in Grand Rapids, MI.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Grand Rapids

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Grand Rapids, MI, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Grand Rapids, MI

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI
