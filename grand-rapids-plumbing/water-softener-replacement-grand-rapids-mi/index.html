    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Grand Rapids, MI

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Michigan homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Grand Rapids

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Grand Rapids

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Grand Rapids

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Grand Rapids

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Grand Rapids

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.
