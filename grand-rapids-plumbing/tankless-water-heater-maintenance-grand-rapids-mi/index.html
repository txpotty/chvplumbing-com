    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Grand Rapids, MI

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Grand Rapids

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Grand Rapids, MI

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Grand Rapids

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Grand Rapids, MI

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Grand Rapids, MI

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Grand Rapids, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Grand Rapids

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.
