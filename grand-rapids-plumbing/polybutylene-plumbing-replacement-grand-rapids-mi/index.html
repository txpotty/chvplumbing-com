    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Grand Rapids

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down.
