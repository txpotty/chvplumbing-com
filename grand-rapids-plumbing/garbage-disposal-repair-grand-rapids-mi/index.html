    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap.     Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Grand Rapids

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Grand Rapids

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Grand Rapids, MI

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. You make the call.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Grand Rapids, MI

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Homes around Grand Rapids on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. Mineral scale traps the food.     Grinding Noise Repair in Grand Rapids

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.
