    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Grand Rapids

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a quieter replacement unit.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly often has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what to keep out of the disposal.
