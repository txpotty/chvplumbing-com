    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Grand Rapids, MI

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Grand Rapids

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink.     Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Grand Rapids

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Grand Rapids, MI

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Grand Rapids

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.
