    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly.     Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Grand Rapids

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not.
