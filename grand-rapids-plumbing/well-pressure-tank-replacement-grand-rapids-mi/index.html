    CHV Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. A rubber bladder inside keeps the air charge apart from the water.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Grand Rapids

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Grand Rapids, MI

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.
