    Workstation Sink Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Grand Rapids

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Grand Rapids, MI

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Grand Rapids, MI

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Grand Rapids, MI

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.
