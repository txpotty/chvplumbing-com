    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Grand Rapids

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. We drain it, disconnect the gas and the vent, carry it out and then install the new tank with fresh connectors, a new drip leg and a properly pitched vent connector. Then we test it. They are dependable and affordable.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Grand Rapids

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. We replace bad sections.

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them.
