    Hydro-Jetting Services in Grand Rapids

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall.     French Drain Installation in Grand Rapids

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. Filter fabric around the stone keeps silt out. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Dry Well Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

    Where there is no place to discharge on the surface, a dry well lets water soak into deeper soil. It only works if the soil drains. We run a percolation check before digging, size the well for the roof or yard area feeding it, and keep it at least ten feet from the foundation and well away from any septic field.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Grand Rapids

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and perimeter drains are a big part of residential plumbing. We pipe downspouts underground in solid PVC to a pop-up emitter or dry well well away from the house, with a cleanout at the base so the line can be flushed. An inch of rain on a 1,000 square foot roof sheds roughly 600 gallons. That is a lot of water.
