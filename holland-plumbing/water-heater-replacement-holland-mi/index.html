    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Holland

    Electric water heaters in Holland usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Holland

    Newer homes mostly rely on copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Holland

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Holland, MI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Holland, MI

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Holland, MI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.
