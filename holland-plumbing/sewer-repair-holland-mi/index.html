    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Holland, MI

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Holland

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Holland

    Every repair starts with a look inside. We run the camera from the cleanout. You get a copy of the video and a plain written summary of each defect with its distance from the house, which helps if you are buying a home, filing a claim or getting a second opinion on a quote someone else gave you. Sometimes the answer is that the line is fine.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Holland

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. We can work outside business hours.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Holland, MI

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Holland, MI

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour.     Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Holland

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.
