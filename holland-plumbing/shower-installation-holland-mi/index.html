    Custom Shower System Installation in Holland, MI

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity.     Shower Stall Installation in Holland

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base.     Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Holland

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.
