    Noritz Descaling in Holland, MI

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Holland

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Holland, MI

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Holland

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Holland

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.
