    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Holland, MI

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in Holland

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. We handle the backflow test and certification paperwork. Trench shoring comes into play below five feet, and we plan the work so the building is without water for hours, not days, often running a temporary supply while the new line is tied in. Night work is possible.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Holland

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Holland

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. Once it has leaked in two different places, patching stops making sense. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, then tie into the meter and the house valve. Most homes are done in a day.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Holland

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. First step, shut off the main valve. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will close the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Holland

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Holland

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Holland

    We start at the meter. With every tap closed, the leak indicator should sit still. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, rule out a running toilet or water softener, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.
