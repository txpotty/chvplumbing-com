    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Holland, MI

    Long, cold winters with heavy snow are the defining stress on plumbing in Michigan, and the heating season can stretch for many months.     Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Holland

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Holland

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Holland, MI

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Holland

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Holland, MI

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Holland
