    Sectional Point Repair in Holland

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Holland, MI

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap. We price both options so you can compare.

    Pipe Bursting in Holland, MI

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Holland

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
