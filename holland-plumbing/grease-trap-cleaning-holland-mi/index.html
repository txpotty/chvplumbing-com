    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Holland, MI

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Holland, MI

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.
