    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Southfield

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Southfield

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Southfield, MI

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Southfield

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Southfield, MI

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Southfield

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Southfield, MI

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. Bonding is a short job. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are some of the best defenses against flooded basements.
