    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet.     Condensing Tankless Replacement in Southfield

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases.     Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Southfield, MI

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside.
