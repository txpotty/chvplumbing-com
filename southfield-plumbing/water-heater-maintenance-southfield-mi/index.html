    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Southfield, MI

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Southfield

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Southfield

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Southfield

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Southfield

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup.     Tank Water Heater Flushing in Southfield

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Southfield, MI

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Southfield, MI use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for proper performance and safety.
