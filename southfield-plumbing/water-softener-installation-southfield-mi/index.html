    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Southfield

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Southfield, MI

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Southfield, MI

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Southfield

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Southfield

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Southfield
