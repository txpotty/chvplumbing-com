    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Southfield, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Southfield

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Southfield

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Southfield

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Southfield, MI

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Southfield

    Spring snowmelt and high groundwater keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Southfield

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Southfield

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.
