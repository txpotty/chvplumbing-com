    Brine Tank Replacement in Southfield

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Use pellet salt.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Southfield

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Southfield

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Southfield, MI

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Southfield, MI

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Michigan homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Southfield

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.
