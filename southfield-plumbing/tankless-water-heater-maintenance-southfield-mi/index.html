    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Southfield

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Southfield, MI

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Southfield, MI

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Southfield

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Southfield

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Descaling in Southfield, MI

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Southfield, MI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Southfield

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.
