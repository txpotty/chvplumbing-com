    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Southfield, MI

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Michigan homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Southfield

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Southfield that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Southfield, MI

    Sewer lines in Southfield, MI can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Southfield, MI

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.
