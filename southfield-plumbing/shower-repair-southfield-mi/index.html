    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Southfield

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Southfield, MI

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Southfield, MI

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Southfield, MI

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. CHV Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Southfield

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.
