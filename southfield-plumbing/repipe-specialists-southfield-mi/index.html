    Understanding the cost of repiping is important for homeowners in Southfield, MI.     Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Southfield

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Southfield

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Southfield.     Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Southfield

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in Southfield varies significantly depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen material (PEX or copper). Generally, you can expect to pay between $4,000 and $15,000 for a PEX repipe, and $8,000 to $25,000 or more for copper. We provide a detailed, itemized quote after an on-site assessment.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Michigan, each with distinct advantages. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally less expensive, making it a great option for many homes. Copper is very durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    CHV Plumbing stands behind our repiping work with a comprehensive warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but generally, you can expect a multi-year warranty covering defects and leaks. We use only quality, warrantied piping materials from trusted brands.

    While some minor wall and ceiling access is usually necessary for a repipe, our repipe specialists use techniques that minimize damage. We carefully cut access points and patch them neatly after the new pipes are installed. We aim to keep disruptions to your home's finishes as minimal as possible during the process.

    We primarily install high-quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in residential applications.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. Water is typically restored each evening.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water. If your home is over 50 years old with original pipes, it's a good idea to have them inspected, especially if they are galvanized or polybutylene.
