    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Southfield, MI

    Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore correct slope.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Southfield

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Southfield, MI

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Together they form dense plugs that a cable may only punch a hole through. Hydro jetting at high pressure scours the pipe walls clean and flushes debris downstream. We also share simple habits that help prevent another grease or wipe clog.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Southfield, MI

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.
