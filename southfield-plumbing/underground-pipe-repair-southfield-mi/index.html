    Most point repairs take a few hours to a day. Replacements take longer. A full service line replacement typically takes one to two days, depending on length, depth and how much of the route can be done with a pull-through method. The schedule comes with the quote.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. Ask them about it. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, and we give you an invoice and repair documentation that shows the date, location and fix. Rules differ by provider.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns that line. The utility owns the meter. The utility usually maintains the meter and the pipe on the street side, and we can help confirm which side of the meter the leak is on before anyone digs. That decides who pays.
