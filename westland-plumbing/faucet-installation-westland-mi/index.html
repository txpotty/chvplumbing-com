    How We Install Faucets in Westland, MI

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Westland, MI

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. We use either a tall deck faucet or a wall spout, sized so water hits near the drain and not the rim where it splashes onto the counter. Spout reach matters too. A short spout means knuckles against the bowl edge.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Westland

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Mineral scale can stick a solenoid open, so we add the inline strainer the maker supplies. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Pot Filler Installation in Westland

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. We tap a cold supply, run it up the wall behind the range and set a shutoff where you can reach it, which code and common sense both want. The arm folds flat when not in use. We pressure-test the line before the backsplash goes up.

    Bathroom Faucet Installation in Westland, MI

    Lavatory faucets in Westland homes mostly come as 4-inch centerset or 8-inch widespread, with single-hole models on newer vanities and vessel tops. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Scale forms fast under a base gasket that is not seated flat. Hole spacing gets measured before you order. A new pop-up drain goes in at the same time, so the finish matches and the old cracked drain gasket is gone.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Westland

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and poorly insulated additions are the most common places for a freeze to cause a break. Where pipes can freeze, we install a frost-free sillcock that closes the valve inside the warm part of the wall. Code calls for a vacuum breaker on every hose bib. Most frost-free models include one, so the backflow protection is built right into the body.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Westland

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. We mount wall or deck models with an integral vacuum breaker, since a hose left in a sink full of soapy water is a textbook backflow risk. Supplies are strapped so they cannot rub.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Westland, MI

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. We bolt the wall bracket into solid backing and fit check valves on both supplies, since a pre-rinse unit gets pulled hard all shift. Hand sinks get wrist blades or sensors. Night installs keep the kitchen open.

    Faucet Upgrades in Westland, MI

    Going from a two-handle faucet to a touchless pull-down changes what is under the sink. You need a dry spot for the control box and either a battery pack or an outlet within reach. We check the outlet before you buy. Filtered water dispensers add a separate line, usually 1/4-inch tubing to a cartridge under the sink.
