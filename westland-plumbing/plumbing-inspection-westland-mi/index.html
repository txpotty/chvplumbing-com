    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale.     Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Westland

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Westland, MI

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout.     Gas Connection Safety Check in Westland, MI

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance.     Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Westland, MI
