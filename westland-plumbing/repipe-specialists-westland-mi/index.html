    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Westland

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Westland, MI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Westland, MI. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Westland

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Westland, MI

    Homes around Westland on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely.
