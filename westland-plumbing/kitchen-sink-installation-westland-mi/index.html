    Workstation Sink Installation in Westland, MI

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Westland, MI

    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Westland, MI

    Farmhouse or apron-front sinks add a distinctive look to any kitchen, but their installation requires specific expertise due to their weight and unique mounting requirements.     Under-mount Sink Installation in Westland, MI

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. CHV Plumbing is skilled at under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a fully sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We confirm the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Westland

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is usually the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Westland

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining well. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.
