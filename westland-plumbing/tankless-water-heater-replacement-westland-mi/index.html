    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Westland

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and musty basements, which can mask slow leaks. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Westland, MI

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting.     Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Westland, MI

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.
