    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Westland

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Westland, MI

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Westland, MI

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Westland

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Westland, MI
