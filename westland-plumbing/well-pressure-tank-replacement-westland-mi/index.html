    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Westland, MI

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Westland

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Westland

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. We shut off power to the pump first. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and plumb it back in with fresh thread sealant and new fittings. Usually a morning.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Westland

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Westland, MI

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, and checking that drawdown matches the pump. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Westland

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Westland

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Water sits right against an air pocket. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, which means draining it to restore the air. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Westland

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Westland

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.
