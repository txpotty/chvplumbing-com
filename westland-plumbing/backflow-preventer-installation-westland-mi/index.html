    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Westland, MI

    Irrigation systems in Westland, MI are a frequent source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants coming from your landscape irrigation.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Westland, MI

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Westland

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Westland

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Westland

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Westland. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CHV Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.
