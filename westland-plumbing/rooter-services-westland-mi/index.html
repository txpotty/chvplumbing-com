    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Westland

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and foul odors coming from drains. If you notice any of these issues, especially multiple fixtures backing up, it's a strong sign you need professional rooter service.

    We use industry-leading equipment for our rooter services. Our technicians commonly use professional-grade tools from brands like RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz for drain augers, sewer cameras, and hydro-jetting machines. These brands are known for their durability and performance in tough plumbing situations.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Westland. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be needed. We will handle all required permitting if that situation arises.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for prompt service with lasting results.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and throw away fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.
