    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Westland

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. A high whistle is different. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Westland

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. The usual failures are the blower motor, the pressure switch and the hot surface igniter.
