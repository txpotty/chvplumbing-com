    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Westland

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Westland

    Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are some of the best defenses against flooded basements. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Westland, MI

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Westland, MI

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Westland

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Westland

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. Our plumber lifts the pump out, cleans the screen and empties the sediment from the bottom. Then we run a full water test. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and perimeter drains are a big part of residential plumbing.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Westland, MI

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Westland, MI

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.
