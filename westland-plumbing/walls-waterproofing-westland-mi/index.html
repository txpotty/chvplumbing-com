    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Westland, MI

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Westland, MI

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Westland, MI

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst.     Basement Wall Sealing in Westland, MI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Westland

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, covering crystalline products, elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Poured concrete takes crystalline coatings well, while block often needs an elastomeric membrane or a drainage board.

    It removes the water mold needs, so new growth slows down. It does not kill mold that is already there. Any mold already growing needs to be remediated before or alongside the waterproofing, and once the space is dry and sealed it should stay that way.
