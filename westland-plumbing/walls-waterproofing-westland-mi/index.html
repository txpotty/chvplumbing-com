    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Westland

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system typically runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing generally ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to ineffective results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our first consultation.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is the smart choice.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Westland may be required. Simple crack repairs usually do not need a permit. CHV Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    CHV Plumbing works with trusted manufacturers known for high-quality waterproofing products. We commonly use products from brands like Xypex, Tremco, and Sika for their proven effectiveness and durability, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. We select the best product for your foundation type and conditions.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for many decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for long-term durability.

    Damp proofing is a less robust solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure.
