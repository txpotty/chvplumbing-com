    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Repair in Pontiac

    Most water heater repairs run between $150 and $600. Thermocouple and element replacements sit at the lower end, while gas control valves and tankless sensors cost more. We quote the exact price before starting.

    While some minor issues might seem DIY-friendly, we highly recommend professional repair, especially for gas water heaters. Incorrect repairs can lead to dangerous situations like gas leaks, electric shocks, or further damage to the unit. Our licensed plumbers deliver safe and effective solutions.

    Most standard water heater repairs, such as replacing a heating element or thermocouple, can be finished within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, like repairing a significant leak or replacing multiple components, might take longer, potentially up to half a day. We aim for efficient service to minimize your inconvenience.

    Some handy homeowners do, but mistakes with gas or 240-volt wiring can be dangerous. A licensed plumber also checks venting and relief valve operation, which DIY repairs usually skip.

    Noises like popping, rumbling, or hissing usually indicate sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank. As the heating element warms the water, it also heats the sediment, causing these sounds. A professional flush and cleaning can usually resolve this issue and improve efficiency.

    Regular maintenance, including annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, checking the anode rod, and inspecting the pressure relief valve, can greatly extend your water heater's lifespan and prevent many common issues. Ask about a maintenance plan with CHV Plumbing.

    A leak from a valve or fitting is urgent but usually fixable. A leak from the tank body can worsen quickly, so shut off the water supply and power or gas and call us right away.

    Rusty or discolored hot water is usually a sign of corrosion inside your water heater tank. This often indicates the anode rod, which protects the tank from rust, needs replacement, or that the tank itself is deteriorating. Addressing this issue quickly can extend the life of your water heater and prevent leaks.
