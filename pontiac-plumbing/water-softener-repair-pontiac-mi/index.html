    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Pontiac, MI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Pontiac, MI

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. CHV Plumbing repairs these units in Pontiac, MI.     Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Pontiac, MI

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.
