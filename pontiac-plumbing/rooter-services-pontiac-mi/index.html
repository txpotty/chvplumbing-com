    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Pontiac

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at CHV Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already weakened, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners, especially for stubborn clogs. These products can damage pipes over time, especially older ones common in Pontiac's established neighborhoods, and often only provide a temporary fix. Professional rooter services are safer and more effective for long-term solutions.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Most standard rooter services for localized clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line root removal or extensive hydro-jetting, may take longer, potentially 3 to 6 hours. We aim to complete the job promptly while staying thorough.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.

    We use top professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and reliability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.
