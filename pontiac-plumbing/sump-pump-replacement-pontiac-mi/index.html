    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Pontiac, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Pontiac, MI

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.
