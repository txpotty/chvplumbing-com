    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Pontiac

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Water sits right against an air pocket. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Pontiac

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. A 20-gallon tank might give you five or six gallons. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Pontiac

    Listen for the switch clicking every few seconds. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Pontiac, MI

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Emergency Pressure Tank Replacement in Pontiac, MI

    If the pump is running nonstop or the taps are dry, call (810) 380-5918. A ruptured tank or a stuck switch can burn out a pump, so we isolate the system, diagnose it and swap the tank, often the same day. Turn off the breaker first.

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.
