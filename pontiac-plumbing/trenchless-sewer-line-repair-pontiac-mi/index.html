    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Pontiac, MI

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Michigan homeowners.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Pontiac

    We use proven trenchless products like those from Perma-Liner, a leader in CIPP technology. Their materials and systems deliver a durable, long-lasting repair that stands up to the demands of local soil and climate conditions. Using trusted brands like Perma-Liner supports the integrity and longevity of our work.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting in Pontiac, MI

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Pontiac

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Pontiac homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
