    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Pontiac

    The cost of garbage disposal replacement in Pontiac, MI usually ranges from $200 to $500, including the new unit and labor. This can vary based on the model chosen, its horsepower, and any necessary plumbing modifications. We provide a clear quote upfront.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is recommended.

    To help it last, avoid putting fibrous or hard items like corn husks, bones, or coffee grounds down the drain. Always run cold water before and after use, and now and then grind citrus peels to keep it fresh. Regular cold water flushing helps maintain the unit.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from two to ten years, and we guarantee our labor.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can cut that life.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually sufficient. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Pontiac home.
