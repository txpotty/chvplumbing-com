    Prefab Shower Replacement in Pontiac

    Prefab units past their useful life usually show it at the base. Fading, small cracks and a spongy floor mean water got through, so the subfloor and framing get checked after removal. Then the new unit goes in. We set it on mortar and seal the drain.

    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Pontiac, MI

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Over enough years, it can lock a cartridge in place, pit a valve body and etch glass doors past the point where cleaning helps, and that is when replacement makes sense. Softened water protects the new shower. Ask us about options.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Upgrades in Pontiac

    Sometimes the shower only needs its guts replaced. If the pan and walls are sound, we can swap an old valve, diverter and trim without tearing out tile, which fixes drips and temperature swings for a fraction of a full rebuild. The difference is real. Half a day, usually.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Pontiac

    Old bathtubs that nobody uses take up the best spot in the bathroom. The tub comes out, the drain is rerouted and the valve goes up to shower height before walls and glass go in. No more tub wall. That is a big deal for many households.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Pontiac

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It is one of the safest changes you can make.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Pontiac, MI

    Grout cracks and failed waterproofing are how tile showers die. Our job is the valve, drain and pan, and we coordinate with the tile installer on the waterproofing membrane. Many installers use Kerdi. It goes on over the backer board.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Pontiac

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. Working from behind the wall when possible, we pull the old valve, install a new one and use a large trim plate to hide the old holes. Tile often stays put. It is a big safety upgrade.

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. We install the new pan with slope and waterproofing, then flood-test it.
