    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Pontiac

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and sewer or water line work generally require a permit and inspection. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Pontiac. This small component offers significant long-term protection.
