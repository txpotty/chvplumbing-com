    Scheduling causes most of the trouble. Pushed pours and framing changes tend to pile phases together. Plan changes after rough-in are the other big one, because moving a tub or adding a bar sink once walls are framed means re-drilling and new inspections. We price changes in writing first.

    PEX-A is our default for water supply in Michigan homes. It tolerates hot water and avoids the pinhole leaks copper can develop in some water. Drains are Schedule 40 PVC. Joints under the slab are kept few, and every turn uses a long sweep so a cable can pass.

    Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Ground movement puts stress on pipes under a slab.
