    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pontiac

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Pontiac yard.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Pontiac, MI

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Then we schedule the fix. When lining fits, the repair gets booked fast, and until then we keep it flowing and tell you what not to send down the drain.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. CHV Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Pontiac

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.
