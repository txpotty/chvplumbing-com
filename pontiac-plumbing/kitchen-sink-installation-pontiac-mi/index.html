    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Pontiac

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Pontiac

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Michigan homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Pontiac efficiently install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Most new kitchen sinks are paired with a disposal and dishwasher. We mount the disposal, connect the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop, and install the dishwasher supply. Doing it all at once saves you time and money. We test every connection under real use.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Pontiac

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Pontiac, MI

    A drop-in sink swap costs less than an undermount on stone or a farmhouse sink with cabinet reinforcement. Adding a disposal, moving the drain or replacing old galvanized stubs adds time.     Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Pontiac

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining well. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.
