    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Pontiac

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Pontiac

    A tankless unit that leaks internally or shows a fault you cannot clear needs prompt attention. We prioritize no-hot-water calls and arrive with common sensors, igniters and filters. If a major component like a heat exchanger has failed, we check warranty coverage and order the part. We can often restore partial operation in the meanwhile.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Pontiac, MI

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to flag specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Pontiac, MI are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This accuracy allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, and more.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Pontiac

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, cutting efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Homes around Pontiac on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Our descaling service in Pontiac involves flushing the unit with a specialized cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is key for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Pontiac

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger.     Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Pontiac
