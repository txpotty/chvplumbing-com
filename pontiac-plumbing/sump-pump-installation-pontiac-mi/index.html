    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Pontiac

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Pontiac, MI

    Sump Pump Replacement in Pontiac, MI

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Pontiac

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Pontiac

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.
