    Emergency Gas Leak Services in Pontiac

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Pontiac

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen.     Emergency Gas Leak Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Hissing at the meter, a sulfur smell in the kitchen, a dead patch of lawn or a carbon monoxide alarm all mean the same thing: get everyone outside before you touch anything.     Appliance Gas Line Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.
