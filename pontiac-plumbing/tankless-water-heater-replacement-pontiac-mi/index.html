    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Pontiac

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Water heaters run harder through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.
