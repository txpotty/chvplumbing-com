    Sump Pump Maintenance in Pontiac, MI: What We Check

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Pontiac

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Pontiac, MI

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Pontiac

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Home Sump Pump Service in Pontiac

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. Our plumber lifts the pump out, cleans the screen and empties the sediment from the bottom. Then we run a full water test. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and foundation drainage are a big part of residential plumbing.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Pontiac

    Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are among the most effective defenses against flooded basements. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Pontiac

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Pontiac

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Pontiac

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.
