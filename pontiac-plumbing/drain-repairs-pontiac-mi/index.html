    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months.     Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Pontiac, MI

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Pontiac

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first.     Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Pontiac, MI
