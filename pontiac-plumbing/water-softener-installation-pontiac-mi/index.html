    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Pontiac

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Pontiac, MI

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Startup matters. Every step gets done.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Pontiac

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Pontiac, MI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Softener Loop Installation in Pontiac

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We cut it in for you. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.
