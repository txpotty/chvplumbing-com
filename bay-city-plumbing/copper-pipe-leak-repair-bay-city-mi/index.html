    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Bay City

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. Every valve gets checked.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Bay City, MI

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Bay City

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Bay City

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Shutdowns cost money. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, and we use press fittings on bigger pipe so the repair goes faster and needs no open flame near occupied spaces. We schedule around business hours.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Bay City

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it. We replace the corroded length. Then we talk about the cause.

    Split Copper Line Repair in Bay City, MI

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Water comes fast. Our plumber isolates the water source, cuts out the split and solders in new pipe, while checking nearby pipe for damage from the same cause. Insulation goes on exposed runs.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Bay City

    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Bay City

    A minor, accessible pinhole repair starts around $200. Hidden leaks cost more. Complex repairs like under-slab leaks can run past $2,000, depending on where the leak is, how bad it is and how much copper needs replacing. We price it after we find it.

    Keep pressure in range. Test it yearly. Soften hard water, limit recirculation pump run time and get any green crust on fittings looked at early, while homes with older copper should have it checked every few years. Early repairs cost less.

    Keep water pressure under about 80 psi. A regulator helps. Treating hard or aggressive water, insulating pipe in cold spots and limiting how long a recirculation pump runs slow down the corrosion that thins copper. Know where your main shutoff is.

    A visible pinhole or a small section is usually done in two to four hours. Hidden leaks add time. Slab leaks or a repipe can take a full day or more, with the method and access setting the pace. We tell you up front.

    Every repair is warrantied. We write the warranty down. Our workmanship warranty covers the repair, while the parts carry separate manufacturer coverage. You see the terms first.

    Wall thickness. K is thickest. Type K is used for underground service lines, Type L is the standard for interior water lines in many codes, while Type M is lighter and more prone to pinholes over time. We repair with Type L.

    A repair clamp or epoxy can buy time in an emergency. It will not last. A plumber should cut out the damaged section and install proper fittings, particularly when the leak is somewhere you cannot see. Shut the water off first.

    Clamps and epoxy putty can stop a drip for a day. Temporary fixes fail. A proper repair means cutting out the thin copper and soldering or pressing in new pipe, and a DIY patch that lets go while you are at work can flood a room in the time it takes to drive home.
