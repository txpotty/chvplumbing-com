    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Bay City

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Michigan homes.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Bay City

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Michigan, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are adept at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining solutions to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Bay City, MI

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Bay City.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bay City, MI

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Bay City.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Bay City, MI

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Bay City, MI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and foundation drainage are a big part of residential plumbing.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Bay City
