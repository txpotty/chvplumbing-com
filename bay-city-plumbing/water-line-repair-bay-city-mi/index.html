    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Bay City

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Bay City

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a clear drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Bay City, reducing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service quickly.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Bay City, MI

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homes around Bay City on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Bay City, MI

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Bay City, MI

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Bay City

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Bay City, MI

    Many older homes in Bay City, MI still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for specific leaks in galvanized pipes where feasible. Our plumbers are adept at working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for future replacement.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Bay City, MI

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired on the same day.
