    Tankless Water Heater Service in Bay City, MI

    Anode Rod Replacement in Bay City

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. Spent rods get replaced. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Bay City

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Bay City

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.
