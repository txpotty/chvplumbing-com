    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing significant blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling noises from pipes, foul odors coming from drains, standing water in sinks or showers, and water backing up into other fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs typically take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more thorough cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.
