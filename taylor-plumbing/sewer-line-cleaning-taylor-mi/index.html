    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Taylor, MI

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Gurgling is another. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Taylor

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Taylor

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. We look for low spots where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the surface, with a copy of the video for you.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Taylor

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Then they spread. Root saws on a sectional cable cut the mass loose, and a jetter follows to remove fine root hairs and bring the pipe back to its full diameter, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Restaurant and Shop Sewer Lines in Taylor, MI

    Commercial lines clog in their own ways. Grease in kitchens. Restaurants battle heavy grease while other businesses see paper, debris or chemical waste, and our heavy-duty equipment handles larger pipe and more severe blockages, working quickly to limit downtime.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Taylor, MI

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Taylor

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. It takes months to close a line. Over time, grease and hair coat the pipe and catch more debris, especially in older cast iron with a rough inner wall, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Commercial Sewer Line Cleaning in Taylor, MI

    Commercial laterals see heavy use. Restaurants fight grease daily. Our larger cables and jetters handle commercial lines, and we work around your hours so customers are not affected.
