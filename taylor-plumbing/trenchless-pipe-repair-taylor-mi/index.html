    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Taylor

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner.     Commercial Trenchless Solutions in Taylor

    Commercial sites in Taylor have longer runs and more laterals. Parking lots sit on top. Schools, offices and stores keep lots and walkways open because we work from cleanouts and manholes, cutting only small pits. We schedule around your hours.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Taylor

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Taylor yard.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Taylor

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Taylor, MI

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. CHV Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.
