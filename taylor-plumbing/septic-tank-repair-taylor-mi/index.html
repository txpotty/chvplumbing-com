    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Taylor, MI

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Tee Repair in Taylor

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Taylor, MI

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Taylor

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and pumping is due once solids fill roughly a third of the tank. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Taylor, MI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Taylor

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
