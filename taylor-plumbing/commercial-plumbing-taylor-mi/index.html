    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Taylor

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. We also check trap primers.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Taylor

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Grease Trap Installation in Taylor

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Taylor

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. You get the video file. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.
