    Slab Leak Reroutes in Taylor

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab.     Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Taylor, MI

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Taylor

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and foundation drainage are a big part of residential plumbing. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Taylor, MI

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we finish.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Taylor, MI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Taylor, MI. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
