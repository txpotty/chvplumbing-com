    Pinhole Leak Repair in Taylor

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Taylor

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Taylor, MI

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, and you pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. Both options get a price.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Taylor

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. We look at venting as well.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Taylor

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. You decide the timing.

    Pipe trouble in a commercial building can close a business for the day. We keep you open.
