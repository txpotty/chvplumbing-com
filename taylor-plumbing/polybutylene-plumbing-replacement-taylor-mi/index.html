    Why Polybutylene Fails in Taylor

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
