    Standard Tank Flushing in Taylor

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Emergency Tank Inspections in Taylor

    Sudden changes call for a look the same day: a tank that suddenly rumbles, hot water that turns brown, a puddle forming under the pan, or a relief valve that will not stop dripping. We diagnose first. Sometimes the fix is a flush and a new drain valve, and sometimes the tank is leaking from the liner and nothing will save it.

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Taylor

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. That alone takes 30 to 40 minutes. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips. Any weeping at the dielectric nipples goes on the report.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Taylor, MI

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. You get a reminder call when the flush is due. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Taylor, MI

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Homes around Taylor on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Taylor, MI

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Most rods last three to five years. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Taylor, MI

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. Past 10 to 12 years, rust at the base or a seam leak means replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.
