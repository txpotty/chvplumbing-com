    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Warren

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Warren, MI

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and CHV Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Parts for the common models ride on the truck. A pump with life left in it is worth repairing, and that saves the cost of a new motor and the labor to set it.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Warren

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Warren, MI

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Michigan code before we restart the pump.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Warren, MI

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Michigan electrical code before power goes back on.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Warren

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Warren

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Warren

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.
