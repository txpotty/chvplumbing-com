    Commercial Leak Detection in Warren, MI

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Warren, MI

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Warren, MI

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.
