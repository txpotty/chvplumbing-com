    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Warren, MI

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done.     Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Warren, MI

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Warren

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.
