    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Warren

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. A 20-gallon tank might give you five or six gallons. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Warren

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.
