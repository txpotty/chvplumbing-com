    Jammed Disposal Repair in Warren

    A humming disposal that will not spin almost always has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button when needed.

    Leaking Garbage Disposal Fix in Warren, MI

    Leaks from a garbage disposal can stem from various points, including loose sink flanges, worn-out seals, cracked housing, or pipe connections.     Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Warren

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Warren

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring. Air switches and batch-feed lids are tested too. Most electrical issues are easy to fix once found.
