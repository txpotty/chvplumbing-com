    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Warren, MI

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Warren and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Warren

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This usually needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Warren

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, especially along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting choices. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.
