    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Warren, MI

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Warren

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out.     Commercial Softener Service in Warren

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Warren

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine.     Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Warren

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward.
