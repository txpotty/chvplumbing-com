    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Warren

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit.     Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Warren

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run.     Root Intrusion Removal in Warren, MI

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Warren yards make this a common call.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Warren

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Warren

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Warren, MI

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Warren we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Warren, MI

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.
