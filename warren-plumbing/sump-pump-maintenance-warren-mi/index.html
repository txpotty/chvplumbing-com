    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Warren, MI

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm.     Backup Pump Battery Service in Warren

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Warren, MI

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house.     Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Warren

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Warren

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Warren

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant.     Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Warren, MI

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff.
