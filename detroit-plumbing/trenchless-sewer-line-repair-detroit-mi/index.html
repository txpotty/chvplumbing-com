    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Detroit

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Detroit that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Detroit, MI

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.
