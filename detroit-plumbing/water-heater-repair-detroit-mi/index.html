    Anode Rod Replacement in Detroit, MI

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Water Heater Brands We Service in Detroit

    Rheem, Bradford White, State and American Standard are the tank brands we see most, with Navien and Rinnai leading on tankless. Each brand has its own weak spots. Our plumbers know them and carry the matching thermocouples, valves and sensors. On a Bradford White, for example, a tripped flammable vapor sensor can look exactly like a dead pilot to someone who has not seen one before.

    Water Heater Maintenance and Tune-Ups in Detroit, MI

    A yearly tune-up adds years to a tank. The visit covers a sediment flush, an anode check, a relief valve test and a look at the connections. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Without flushing, that sediment hardens into a layer that no garden hose will move.

    Repair vs Replace Cost Comparison in Detroit, MI

    A typical repair costs $150 to $600, while a new standard tank installed often runs $1,400 to $2,800 depending on size, fuel and code updates. For a heater younger than eight years with a good tank, repair usually wins. Past ten years, or with a leaking tank, replacement saves money. We give you both prices.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Detroit

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    No Hot Water Repair in Detroit, MI

    Check the breaker first. On an electric tank, a tripped breaker or a popped high-limit reset button is the easiest fix there is. On gas units we work through the pilot, then the thermocouple, then the gas valve. Most calls end in under two hours.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Detroit, MI

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. We check the thermostat setting and the house pressure first. On homes with a pressure regulator, a missing expansion tank is often the real problem. Leaking fittings get redone.

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. Lukewarm water that runs out fast usually means the lower element. We test resistance with a meter. The replacement parts ride on the truck, which keeps it to one visit.
