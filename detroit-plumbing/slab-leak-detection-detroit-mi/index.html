    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Detroit, MI

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Detroit

    Single-family homes and townhouses across Detroit get the same step-by-step process. Meter test first. We isolate, trace and listen, then confirm with a thermal scan or tracer gas, then explain what we found and give you written repair prices.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Detroit

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Detroit homes.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Detroit, MI

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Detroit, MI

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, repair the section with approved fittings and pressure test it. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. A precise mark keeps the opening tight.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Detroit

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Detroit

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.
