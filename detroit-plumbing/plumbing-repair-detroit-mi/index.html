    Common Plumbing Repairs in Detroit, MI

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Detroit, MI

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. A camera and locator mark exactly where and how deep the break sits. Repair options run from a traditional dig to pipe bursting or a cured-in-place liner. We camera it again before backfilling.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Detroit, MI

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Both skip the trench through your driveway and landscaping. Once restoration is counted, the price is often close to a deep dig. A camera decides.

    Slab Leak Repair in Detroit, MI

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a bill that doubles for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera before anyone breaks concrete. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute overhead, each priced separately.

    Gas Pipe Repair in Detroit, MI

    Leave the house first if you smell gas, and call the gas utility from outside. After the utility makes it safe, we come in for the repair. We pressure test the system, track the leak with an electronic detector and soap solution, and replace failed flex connectors, shutoff valves or corroded steel pipe. Nothing is relit until the test holds.

    Slow Drain Repair in Detroit

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. No monthly snaking.

    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Detroit

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. Sulfur smell on the hot side points to the anode rod. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. Rust at the seams is different. That tank is done, and we will say so.
