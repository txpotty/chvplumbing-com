    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Detroit

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Detroit, MI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Detroit, MI. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Detroit, MI

    Homes around Detroit on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely.
