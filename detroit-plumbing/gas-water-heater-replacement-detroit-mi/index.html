    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Detroit

    Most gas water heater replacements in Detroit, MI cost $1,200 to $3,500. Tank size and brand set most of it, and gas line or venting upgrades can push it toward the top of that range. You get a written price first.

    Annual flushing and an anode check every few years are the main things. Most gas tanks die from sediment, which sits on the burner side and cooks the steel until it fails. Have it looked at now and then.

    Tank warranties usually run 6 to 10 years, with 1 to 2 years on parts like the gas valve and igniter. Our labor warranty covers the install. Hang on to your paperwork.

    Flush the tank once a year. Draining a few gallons from the valve at the bottom clears sediment before it hardens, and checking the anode rod every three years tells you when to replace it before the tank starts to rust. Proper sizing helps too.

    Most swaps run three to five hours. Converting to a power vent or moving the heater to a new spot can take most of a day, since it involves new vent and gas piping. We tell you the timeline first.

    Eight to twelve years for most gas tanks. Sediment from hard water and big temperature swings in an uninsulated garage can shorten that by a few years or more. Annual flushing and anode checks stretch it out.

    Gas and venting mistakes are the dangerous part. A loose flare fitting leaks gas, and a vent connector without upward slope can spill carbon monoxide into the house while everything looks normal. It is a job for a licensed pro.
