    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Detroit, MI

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Detroit.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Detroit

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age.
