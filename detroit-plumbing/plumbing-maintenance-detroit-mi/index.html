    Water Heater Maintenance in Detroit

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Detroit, MI

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your choices.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Detroit, MI

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still smart. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and poorly insulated additions are the most common places for a freeze to cause a break. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Detroit, MI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Detroit

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections.
