    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Detroit

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Detroit

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Detroit, MI

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Detroit, MI

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.
