    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Detroit, MI

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front.     Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Detroit, MI

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.
