    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Detroit, MI

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Detroit, MI provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it usually leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more long-term solution to your drainage issues.

    Main Sewer Line Jetting in Detroit, MI

    The main line from your home to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups in multiple fixtures. We jet from the nearest cleanout and work the nozzle all the way to the city tap. Afterward, a camera inspection shows whether the line is clean or has structural damage. Clean lines also give a clearer picture for any future sewer repairs.
