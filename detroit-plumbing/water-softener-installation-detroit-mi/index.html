    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Detroit, MI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Detroit, MI

    Building a home in Detroit, MI? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. Builders call us in early. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Detroit

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one.     Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Detroit, MI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. CHV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Detroit

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Detroit

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.
