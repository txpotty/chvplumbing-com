    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Detroit, MI

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition.     Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Detroit, MI

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Older neighborhoods may also have lead service lines, which the state is requiring utilities to replace over time.
