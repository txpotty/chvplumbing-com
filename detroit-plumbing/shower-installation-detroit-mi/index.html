    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Detroit

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Detroit, MI

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Detroit, MI

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Both prevent scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Detroit, MI

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. We work with your tile setter on the waterproofing membrane and the slope, since water has to reach the drain before it can roll out the open side of the shower. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. We pick the pan system to match.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Detroit, MI

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Then the rough-in inspection. We return for trim after tile.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Detroit

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Detroit, MI

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Most installs finish in a day.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Detroit

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.
