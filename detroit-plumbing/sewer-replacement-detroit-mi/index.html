    Sewer Line Replacement Options in Detroit, MI

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Detroit

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Detroit

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and coordinate traffic control if the tap sits in the street, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Detroit, MI

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, and a clean pipe gives a much more honest picture of its condition. Guesswork costs money.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Detroit, MI

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Detroit, MI

    Plenty of older houses still drain through their first sewer pipe. Clay joints open up and let roots in, cast iron scales and cracks from the inside, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid 1900s, slowly flattens until almost nothing passes. Replacement means PVC or HDPE.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Detroit

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Detroit

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Detroit

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. PVC avoids all three of those problems. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Local Sewer Replacement Near Me in Detroit, MI

    Local plumbers know local sewer layouts. Older lots sometimes drain to an alley main instead of the street. That affects depth, length and which method fits, and in Detroit we often know it before the truck pulls up. We still camera everything.

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. On sewer jobs that is a big deal. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in Detroit around the inspector's visit and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.
