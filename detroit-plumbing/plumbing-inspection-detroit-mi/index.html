    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Detroit, MI

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Detroit, MI

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Detroit

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Many buyers use our findings to ask for repairs or credits, and the sewer video alone has saved buyers from five-figure surprises.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Detroit, MI

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Detroit

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Detroit

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Detroit, MI

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Detroit, MI

    Older homes in Detroit often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Detroit

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. Findings help with negotiations. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Detroit

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Repairs may need one. If the inspection leads to repairs or replacements that need a permit, we will say so up front and handle it if we do the job.

    A plumbing inspection is a deeper look at one system. Home inspectors cover the basics. Home inspectors run water and flush toilets, and our plumbers add sewer video, pressure tests, pipe identification and code review.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. If your home has original supply pipe, large trees near the sewer or a history of leaks, a yearly inspection catches problems while they are still small and cheap to fix.
