    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Detroit, MI

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Detroit, MI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Detroit, MI

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Leaks only get worse.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Detroit

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Detroit, MI

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We help you compare.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Detroit

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. Registration matters.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Detroit

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Detroit, MI

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Detroit, MI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
