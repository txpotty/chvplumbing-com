    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Detroit

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Detroit

    A like-for-like gas tankless replacement usually runs $2,800 to $4,500 installed, depending on the brand and venting. Converting from a tank to tankless often costs $4,000 to $6,500 because of gas line and venting upgrades. We give you a written quote before any work starts.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to additional plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is usually not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to dangerous gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like CHV Plumbing.

    Most jurisdictions in Michigan require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include repeated error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming frequent and costly, replacement is often the more sensible choice.

    Frequent error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is typically the better value.

    New tankless water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the heat exchanger, usually 10 to 15 years, and on parts, usually 3 to 5 years. CHV Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation workmanship. We will review all warranty details with you before your replacement so you understand your coverage.
