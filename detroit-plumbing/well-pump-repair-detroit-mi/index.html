    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Detroit, MI

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Detroit, MI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Detroit, MI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Spring snowmelt and a rising water table keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Detroit

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Same-Day Well Pump Repair in Detroit

    Hot weather makes a dry house worse. No-water calls get a fast response and, when parts allow, a same-day fix. A temporary setup can sometimes cover the house while a pump is on order. Our dispatcher can walk you through checking the breaker and the pressure switch over the phone while the truck is on the way.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Detroit, MI

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and CHV Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Parts for the common models ride on the truck. Keeping a pump that still has life left saves you the cost of a new motor and the labor of setting it.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Detroit

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Detroit

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Detroit

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.
