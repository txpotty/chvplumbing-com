    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We confirm heights with your contractor first. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Detroit

    Commercial PEX work in Detroit gets scheduled around your hours.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Detroit

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Detroit, MI

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Detroit, MI

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Replacing it with PEX can make the house easier to insure and sell.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Detroit

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loop length matters as well. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Detroit, MI

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe.
