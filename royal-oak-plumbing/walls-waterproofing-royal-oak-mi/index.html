    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Royal Oak, MI

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Royal Oak

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during heavy storms. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Royal Oak

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Long, cold winters with heavy snow are the defining stress on plumbing in Michigan, and the heating season can stretch for many months. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use tough, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Royal Oak, MI

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Royal Oak

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks early in Royal Oak homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Royal Oak, MI

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.
