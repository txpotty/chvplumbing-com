    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Royal Oak

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of caustic chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that prevents a very expensive one.

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Royal Oak

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a properly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Royal Oak

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you plainly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once we find it.
