    Well Pump Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. A submersible pull takes two techs and a truck.

    Emergency Plumbing Repair in Royal Oak

    Burst pipes and overflowing toilets do not keep business hours, so our phones are answered around the clock. The first job is stopping the water, usually at the main shutoff or the fixture valve. After that we make a lasting repair or a safe temporary one so you have water back tonight and a permanent fix scheduled. Keep a shutoff key handy.

    Sewer Line Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up on a regular schedule. They slip into a cracked joint, then grow into a mat that snags paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Water Leak Repair in Royal Oak

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down. We open just enough drywall or dirt to make the repair. Fixes include pinholed copper, failed PEX crimps, leaking hose bibs and worn shutoff valves.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a water bill that jumps for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. Acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera find the spot before anyone touches the concrete. The repair might be a spot dig through the floor or a reroute through the attic, and you get a price for each.

    Slow Drain Repair in Royal Oak

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. After clearing it, we camera the line and fix whatever is broken. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Royal Oak

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. CHV Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Royal Oak, MI

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Neither one needs a trench across the driveway. Counting concrete and sod replacement, the cost often lands near an excavation. A camera decides.
