    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Royal Oak

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and sewer or water line work generally require a permit and inspection. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Royal Oak. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Royal Oak

    The cost of water heater installation in Royal Oak, MI can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    CHV Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    A traditional tank water heater usually lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.
