    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Royal Oak, MI: The Details

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Royal Oak, MI

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Royal Oak

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Royal Oak

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Royal Oak

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. Many farmhouse sinks drain at the back, and the trap has to work around that.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Royal Oak, MI

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Royal Oak, MI

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.
