    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Royal Oak

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Royal Oak, MI

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Royal Oak, MI

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank.     Choosing a Toilet Brand in Royal Oak, MI

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts.     Toilet Brands We Replace in Royal Oak

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Royal Oak, MI

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard.
