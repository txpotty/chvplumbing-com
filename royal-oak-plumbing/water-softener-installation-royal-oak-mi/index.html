    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Royal Oak

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Royal Oak home. This ensures every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Royal Oak

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps performing.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Royal Oak, MI

    Salt-based water softeners are the most common and effective type, using ion exchange to remove hard minerals like calcium and magnesium. CHV Plumbing specializes in the installation of these traditional systems, making sure of proper brine tank setup and regeneration cycles. We can recommend efficient models that minimize salt and water consumption for your Royal Oak, MI home.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Royal Oak, MI

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin.     Softener Loop Installation in Royal Oak
