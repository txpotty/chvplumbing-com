    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Royal Oak, MI

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected.     Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Royal Oak

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Royal Oak, MI

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods.
