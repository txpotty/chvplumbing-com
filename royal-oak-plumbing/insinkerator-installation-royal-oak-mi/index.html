    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Royal Oak, MI

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Badger Series Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Badger Disposal Install in Royal Oak

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. We hang it on the quick-lock mount, connect the discharge tube and add a cord kit if the old unit was plugged in, then run a test grind with cold water running to clear the chamber. About an hour, usually.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Royal Oak

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We mount the unit and plumb the water feed, and your electrician handles the circuit. Health inspectors look at these. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we check that rule before the job starts.
