    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Royal Oak, MI

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Royal Oak

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Royal Oak

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Royal Oak, MI

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. We size for that spike. Shared delivery-only kitchens can have more sinks per square foot than any dining room, and the trap must handle all of them. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Royal Oak

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. Our sizing starts from your peak flow, the worst ten minutes of the night, rather than a daily average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. We set the unit, run the inlet and outlet in schedule 40 PVC or cast iron, and add the flow control fitting the manufacturer calls for.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Royal Oak

    School cafeterias and hospital kitchens cook in big batches and wash in big batches, which means large pot sinks and a dish machine running hard for two hours after each meal. Most end up with an outdoor gravity interceptor. We schedule around the calendar. For schools that often means digging over a break, and for hospitals it means phasing the tie-in so the kitchen never fully shuts down.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Royal Oak, MI

    Price follows size and location more than brand. A small indoor trap set on an open floor near the sink is the cheapest version of this job. The high end is an outdoor gravity interceptor with excavation, a traffic-rated lid and a permit, which can run five figures once concrete restoration is counted. We quote after a site visit.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Royal Oak, MI

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. The unit goes on the floor or in a shallow recess, and the flow control fitting goes upstream. Clearance matters most. Your staff needs to lift the lid and clean it weekly, so we leave space to do that without unbolting anything.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. With a plastic tank in wet ground we add anti-flotation anchoring, because an empty tank in saturated soil can pop right out of the hole.

    Watch the pump-out bills. Rising service frequency means the trap has been outgrown or the baffles have failed. Smell is another clue.
