    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Royal Oak

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Royal Oak, MI

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Homes around Royal Oak on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Royal Oak

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Royal Oak, MI

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Royal Oak, MI

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Root Removal Jetting in Royal Oak

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Royal Oak, MI

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are some of the best defenses against flooded basements. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Royal Oak

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.
