    Picking the Right Softener for Royal Oak, MI Homes

    Metered Softener Replacement in Royal Oak

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Royal Oak

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Royal Oak, MI

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Royal Oak

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Royal Oak

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Softener Loop Installation in Royal Oak, MI

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Royal Oak

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Royal Oak, MI

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.
