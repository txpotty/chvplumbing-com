    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Ann Arbor

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. The old flush valve comes off, the supply gets capped behind the wall, and the new waterless bowl goes on the same carrier with its cartridge housing tied into the existing drain.     Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Ann Arbor

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Ann Arbor, MI

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.
