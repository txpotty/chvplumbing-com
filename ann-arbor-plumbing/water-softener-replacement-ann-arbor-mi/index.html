    Fleck Valve Programming in Ann Arbor, MI

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Ann Arbor

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Or the whole system.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Ann Arbor, MI

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Ann Arbor, MI

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. You see the choices on one page.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Ann Arbor

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Ann Arbor, MI

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Ann Arbor

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Ann Arbor

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.
