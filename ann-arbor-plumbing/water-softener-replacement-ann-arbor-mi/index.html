    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Ann Arbor

    The cost of water softener replacement in Ann Arbor, MI varies based on the type of system, its capacity and installation complexity. Generally, homeowners can expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000 for a quality unit and professional installation. We provide clear, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can signal a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from CHV Plumbing. We back our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank.     While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components.
