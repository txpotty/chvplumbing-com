    Steam Shower Plumbing in Ann Arbor

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit.     Digital Shower Systems in Ann Arbor, MI

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Ann Arbor

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Installation in Ann Arbor

    Shower plumbing for a remodel, including valve, drain and trim, usually costs $1,200 to $3,500. Full walk-in showers with tile, glass and plumbing often total $8,000 to $20,000 or more, with most of that in tile and glass rather than plumbing. Prefab units cost less.

    Large rain heads and multiple outlets use more hot water. We look at the heater's size and recovery rate and tell you if it needs an upgrade.

    Shower plumbing gets buried behind tile. Mistakes like a loose drain clamp or bad slope hide until water damage appears in a ceiling or floor. Fixing it means tearing out tile.

    Center drains and linear drains cover most showers. Center drains need four-way slope, and linear drains allow a single slope that works well with big tile. Tile size often decides.

    Our workmanship warranty typically runs one year. The shower unit and fixtures carry their own warranties, from one year up to lifetime. We go over it before work starts.
