    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Ann Arbor

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Ann Arbor, MI

    Foundation Crack Repair in Ann Arbor

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Ann Arbor

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and foundation drainage are a big part of residential plumbing. Backfill goes in last.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Ann Arbor, MI

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago.
