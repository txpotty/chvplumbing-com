    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Ann Arbor

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and damp basements, which can mask slow leaks. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes.
