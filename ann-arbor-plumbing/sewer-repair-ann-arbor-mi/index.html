    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Ann Arbor, MI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Ann Arbor

    The cost of sewer repair in Ann Arbor, MI can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 for extensive trenchless work, and up to $10,000 or more for full trench-based sewer line replacement. Factors like the extent of damage, type of repair, pipe material, depth and accessibility all influence the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a thorough inspection.

    Sewer repair is a complex job that requires specialized tools, expertise and adherence to plumbing codes. Attempting DIY sewer repair can lead to further damage, costly mistakes and potential health hazards. It's always recommended to hire a licensed professional like CHV Plumbing so the job is done correctly and safely, especially in Ann Arbor where permits may be required.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method that allows for pipe repair or replacement without extensive digging. Techniques like pipe lining, also called cured-in-place pipe, or pipe bursting minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping. It's an great option for preserving your property while fixing underground pipes.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another common warning sign.

    A small rental auger can clear a soft clog, but it will not fix a crack, belly or root intrusion. Forcing a cable through a broken pipe can make the damage worse. A camera inspection tells you whether you have a clog or a structural problem.

    The duration of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This protects your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    To prevent future sewer line problems, avoid flushing anything other than toilet paper, dispose of grease properly and be mindful of what goes down your drains. Consider having yearly professional drain cleaning or sewer inspections, especially if you have older pipes or mature trees near your sewer line. Regular maintenance can identify small issues before they become major problems.
