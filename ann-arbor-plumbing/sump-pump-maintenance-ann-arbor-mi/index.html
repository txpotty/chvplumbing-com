    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Ann Arbor

    Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Ann Arbor

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Ann Arbor, MI

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Ann Arbor, MI

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Ann Arbor, MI.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Ann Arbor, MI

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, CHV Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Ann Arbor, MI.     Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Ann Arbor

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Ann Arbor

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage.
