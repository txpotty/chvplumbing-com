    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Ann Arbor, MI

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one.     Hard Water Damage Assessment in Ann Arbor

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.
