    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Ann Arbor

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Ann Arbor with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Ann Arbor, MI

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Ann Arbor, MI

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Michigan plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Ann Arbor home.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Ann Arbor

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Detection in Ann Arbor

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Ann Arbor, MI

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Ann Arbor, MI.
