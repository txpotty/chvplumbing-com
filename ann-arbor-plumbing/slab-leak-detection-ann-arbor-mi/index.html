    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Ann Arbor

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Ann Arbor

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Ann Arbor

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Ann Arbor home.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Ann Arbor, MI

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Ann Arbor home.
