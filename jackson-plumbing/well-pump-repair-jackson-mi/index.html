    Dependable Well Pump Repair Services in Jackson, MI

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Jackson

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Jackson, MI

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Jackson, MI

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Jet Pump Repair in Jackson, MI

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Jackson is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Jackson, MI

    Franklin Electric is a leading manufacturer of well pumps and motors, known for their durability. Our technicians are highly familiar with Franklin Electric products, from submersible motors to control boxes. We carry common Franklin Electric replacement parts to ensure efficient repairs. Whether it's a motor rewind or a complete pump replacement, you can count on our expertise with Franklin Electric systems in Jackson.
