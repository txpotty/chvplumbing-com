    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Jackson, MI

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Jackson, MI

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Jackson

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Jackson

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Jackson, MI

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Jackson, MI

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Jackson

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.
