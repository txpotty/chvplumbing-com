    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Jackson

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Jackson

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Jackson

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Jackson, MI

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Jackson, MI

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Jackson, MI

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.
