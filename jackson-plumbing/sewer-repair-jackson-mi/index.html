    What Sewer Repair Covers in Jackson, MI

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Jackson

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Jackson

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Michigan homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Jackson

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Jackson

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Jackson

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Jackson often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Jackson

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Jackson

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Jackson, MI

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.
