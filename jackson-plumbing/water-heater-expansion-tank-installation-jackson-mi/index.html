    Brands and Tank Materials in Jackson, MI

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Stainless steel connections resist corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Jackson, MI

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Jackson

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are common. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you stable pressure throughout the house.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Jackson

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.
