    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Jackson, MI

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Jackson, MI

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Jackson, MI

    A 1 HP disposal earns its keep in a busy kitchen, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Jackson

    On new builds and remodels in Jackson, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.
