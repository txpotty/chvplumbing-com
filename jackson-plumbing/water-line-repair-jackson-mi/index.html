    Underground Leak Detection in Jackson, MI

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Once the spot is marked, you get a written repair price before we dig.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Jackson, MI

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Jackson

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Jackson, MI

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time.
