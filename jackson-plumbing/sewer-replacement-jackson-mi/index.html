    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Jackson

    Brittle pipe bursts cleanly. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Jackson

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Jackson, MI

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. Each of those joints is an opening for roots. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Jackson

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Jackson, MI

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Jackson, MI

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop using sinks, toilets and the washer. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Jackson

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Jackson, MI

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.
