    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Jackson, MI

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Jackson

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, so a torn bladder or rusted seam means a new tank, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Jackson, MI

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Jackson

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Shut off the breaker to the pump. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Jackson

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Jackson

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. We shut off power to the pump first. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and connect it with new fittings and fresh sealant. Usually a morning.

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Jackson

    We keep common tank sizes on our trucks. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Jackson, MI

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Jackson, MI

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Moisture eats steel tanks from the bottom. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.
