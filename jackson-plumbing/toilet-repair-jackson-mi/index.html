    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Jackson

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall.     Clogged Toilet Clearing in Jackson, MI

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Jackson, MI

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Jackson

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. Put the wrong flapper on one and it will leak within weeks. We read the model number cast inside the tank wall and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Jackson, MI

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Jackson

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The water level drops until the fill valve kicks on again. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Jackson

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.
