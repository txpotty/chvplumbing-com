    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Jackson, MI

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. The burner comes out, the ports get brushed and the orifice gets cleared.     Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Jackson

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Jackson, mostly in basements and utility closets. Parts for them are simpler, and a universal thermocouple fits most.     Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Jackson

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.
