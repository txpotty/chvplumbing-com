    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Port Huron

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub tend to mean something is stuck in the line before the tank.     Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Port Huron, MI

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Port Huron, MI

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Kitchen grease and restroom paper towels cause most of the trouble. Our fix often includes a bigger effluent filter and a pumping schedule tied to real use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Port Huron

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Septic Tee Repair in Port Huron

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.
