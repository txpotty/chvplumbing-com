    Jetting a Sewer Line in Port Huron, MI

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Port Huron, MI

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Over years the opening shrinks. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Port Huron

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Port Huron

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.
