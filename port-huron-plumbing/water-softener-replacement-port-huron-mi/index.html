    Swapping a salt-based softener in Michigan homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Port Huron

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again.
