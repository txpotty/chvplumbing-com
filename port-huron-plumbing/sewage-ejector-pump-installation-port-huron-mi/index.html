    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Port Huron

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Port Huron

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Port Huron, MI

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Port Huron

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. Old switches fail first.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Port Huron, MI

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. Cracked basins get replaced.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Port Huron

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are some of the best defenses against flooded basements. Battery backup is an option.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Port Huron, MI
