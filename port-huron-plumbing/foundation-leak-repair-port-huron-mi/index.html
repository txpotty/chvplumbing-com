    Common signs of a foundation leak include sudden increases in water bills, wet spots on floors or walls, mildew odors, visible cracks in the foundation or interior walls, and standing water around the foundation perimeter. If you notice any of these, it is important to have your foundation inspected promptly.

    Warm or damp spots on the floor, cracks in tile or concrete, mildew under carpet, the sound of running water and a water meter that moves with all fixtures off are common signs. Higher water bills are often the first clue.

    Foundation leaks can be caused by various factors, including shifting soil that stresses pipes, corrosion of older copper lines from water or soil chemistry, poor pipe installation, and even large temperature swings. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Homes around Port Huron on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely.

    While not always preventable, regular plumbing inspections can help identify potential weaknesses.
