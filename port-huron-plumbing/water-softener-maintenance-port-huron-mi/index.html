    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Port Huron, MI

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Port Huron, MI

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Port Huron, MI

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Port Huron, MI

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold.
