    Sewer Pipe Relining in Port Huron, MI

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Port Huron.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Port Huron

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age.
