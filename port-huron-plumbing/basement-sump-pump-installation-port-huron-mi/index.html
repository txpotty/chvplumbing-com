    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Port Huron

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Port Huron homes. Expect more noise.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Port Huron

    No pit yet? First we locate the low point where water gathers, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Port Huron, MI

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Port Huron, MI

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Port Huron, MI we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.
