    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Port Huron

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Port Huron, MI

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Port Huron, MI

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Port Huron

    Water heater installation in Port Huron, MI runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. Type, capacity and fuel set most of the price, and any gas line, venting or electrical upgrades add to it. Tankless costs more. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    Age, repair cost and efficiency decide it. If the heater is over ten years old or the repair costs more than half of a new one, replacing it usually makes more sense. Tank leaks cannot be fixed.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Homes with a closed plumbing system are often required by code to have one, since it protects the heater and every pipe in the house.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It protects the heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    The job involves gas, venting, electrical and pressure safety. A licensed plumber installs it safely and protects your warranty.

    Our crews install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz and Navien. If you want to stick with your current brand, we can usually get it.
