    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Port Huron

    Submersible pumps are common for deeper wells, as they operate submerged within the well casing.     Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    Not every well failure means a bad pump. Failed capacitors, relays or pressure switches can stop the system. We test these components first and replace them if needed. This can save you the cost of pulling the pump. We explain what we find before making recommendations.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Port Huron, MI

    Jet pumps are usually used for shallower wells and are located above ground, drawing water up through a suction pipe. Common issues include motor failure, impeller damage or pressure switch failures. CHV Plumbing provides professional jet pump replacement with correct sizing and pressure tank integration. We make sure your new jet pump delivers steady water pressure throughout your Port Huron, MI property.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Port Huron

Often, when a well pump is replaced, the pressure tank is also nearing the end of its life. CHV Plumbing can replace your well pressure tank alongside your pump.
