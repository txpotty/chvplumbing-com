    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Sterling Heights

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Sterling Heights, MI

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Sterling Heights, MI

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Sterling Heights, MI

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.
