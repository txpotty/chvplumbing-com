    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Sterling Heights

    Most poly replacements in Sterling Heights fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price. A small single-bath home with easy attic access sits at the low end, while a large two-story house with several bathrooms, or one where we choose copper, sits at the high end. We give you an itemized price after the inspection.

    Properly installed PEX usually lasts 25 to 50 years. Copper often goes further. Type L copper can last 50 years or more in friendly water, and either one is a large step up from polybutylene.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty on the repipe, and pipe makers offer their own long-term warranties. We explain coverage before work begins.

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Hot lines and fittings usually go before the rest.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. We use Uponor most often. For copper, we install Type L tubing from established mills rather than the thinner Type M found in many older homes, because the thicker wall holds up better over time.

    Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Larger homes take longer. Fixture count, attic access and pipe choice set the schedule, and you will usually have water each evening.

    Both are good choices. PEX usually costs less. PEX is flexible, handles freezing and movement better and installs faster, while copper is rigid, tolerates heat and sunlight and has a long track record, so the right answer depends on budget, water and preference.

    Our poly replacements carry a written warranty on materials and labor. Terms are on your estimate. Certified PEX-A installs also carry the pipe manufacturer's long-term warranty, and we register it for you so the paperwork is on file if you ever need it.
