    Backflow Testing and Certification in Sterling Heights

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage.     Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Sterling Heights

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp.
