    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Sterling Heights

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    CHV Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. CHV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all influence the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.
