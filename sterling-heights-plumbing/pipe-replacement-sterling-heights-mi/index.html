    PEX Repiping in Sterling Heights

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.
