    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Sterling Heights, MI

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Sterling Heights, MI

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Sterling Heights

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Turn the pump breaker off right away. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Sterling Heights, MI

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. The difference between two tank sizes is usually modest. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Sterling Heights, MI

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Sterling Heights

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Sterling Heights

    Our vans carry the sizes most homes use. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Sterling Heights

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Sterling Heights, MI

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.
