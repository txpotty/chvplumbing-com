    Standard Disposal Installation in Sterling Heights

    Most households in Sterling Heights do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Sterling Heights, MI

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Sterling Heights

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Sterling Heights, MI

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
