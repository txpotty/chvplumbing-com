    Foundation Leak Detection in Sterling Heights

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Sterling Heights, MI

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Sterling Heights

    Modern homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation. CHV Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use proper fusion and solvent welding techniques to make strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Sterling Heights foundation.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Sterling Heights, MI

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to find cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We explain the trade-offs of each method.

    Water Line Reroute in Sterling Heights

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.
