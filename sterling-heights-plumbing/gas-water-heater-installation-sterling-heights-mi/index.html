    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Sterling Heights

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas tank usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless units run higher due to vent and gas work.

    A conventional heater keeps a tank of hot water ready, which can run out. A tankless unit heats water as it flows, so it never runs out, but it needs more involved venting and a larger gas line.

    We install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai. Our pick depends on warranty and use.

    Once a year is the right interval for a gas heater. Yearly service catches burner, anode and relief valve problems early.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment over the burner makes the tank run hot and noisy, and a spent anode lets the steel rust from the inside. We offer yearly maintenance.
