    Sump Pump Maintenance in Sterling Heights, MI: What We Check

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Sterling Heights

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Sterling Heights

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Sterling Heights

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Sterling Heights

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Sterling Heights

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Sterling Heights, MI

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Sterling Heights

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Float Switch Inspection in Sterling Heights, MI

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Sump Pump Maintenance Near Me in Sterling Heights, MI

    Water pools in predictable places around a house: the downspout corners, the low side of the yard and wherever the grade slopes back toward the wall. We look at all of that while we are outside checking the discharge. A little regrading can cut the pump's workload.

    Low spots on a block flood first, and our crews know which Sterling Heights streets see standing water after a hard storm. That tells us where discharge lines need extra length. Pumps in low lots cycle far more often.
