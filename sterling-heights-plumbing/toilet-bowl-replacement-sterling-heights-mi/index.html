    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Sterling Heights

    Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. Water left sitting in a toilet in an unheated cabin or vacant house can freeze, and the ice cracks the bowl at the trap where you cannot see it. Toilets in unheated spaces should be drained. We replace the cracked bowl and show you how to winterize, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Sterling Heights, MI

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old cast iron can crack if you pry on it. Prying on the flange is something we avoid and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Sterling Heights, MI

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Sterling Heights

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. We match the outlet and spud size. We reconnect the flushometer and test it before the restroom reopens.

    Emergency Toilet Bowl Replacement in Sterling Heights, MI

    A bowl that splits open can dump the tank and every refill onto the floor until someone closes the angle stop. Close it first. With the water off, the broken bowl comes out, in pieces if it has to, wrap the shards and set a replacement or a full new toilet as fast as we can get one.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Sterling Heights, MI

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Old cast iron flanges tend to rust through. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Sterling Heights

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order. We coordinate with your contractor or tile crew so the flange ends up at the right height above the finished floor.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Sterling Heights

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Some bowls are not sold on their own. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.
