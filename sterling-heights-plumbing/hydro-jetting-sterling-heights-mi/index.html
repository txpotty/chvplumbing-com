    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Sterling Heights

    Residential hydro jetting in Sterling Heights usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    For most homes in Sterling Heights, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may need quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include regularly recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often indicate a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to fully scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    CHV Plumbing backs our hydro jetting in Sterling Heights, MI. We usually offer a warranty on our workmanship, with the length depending on the service and nature of the clog. We will discuss all warranty details with you before starting any work.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Sterling Heights usually takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.
