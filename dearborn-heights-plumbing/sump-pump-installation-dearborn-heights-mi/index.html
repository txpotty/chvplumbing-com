    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly.     Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Dearborn Heights

    A pump swap in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. New pits, with saw cutting, excavation and a concrete patch, run about $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups add $500 to $1,500.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. Heavy use shortens that. A quick inspection tells us where it stands.

    Not every home needs one. Homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or yards that collect runoff get the most from a pump, since storm water has nowhere else to go.

    Manufacturer warranties usually run three to five years, longer on premium models. Our labor carries its own warranty.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. Anything involving a new pit, a new discharge route or new wiring is a job for a professional.
