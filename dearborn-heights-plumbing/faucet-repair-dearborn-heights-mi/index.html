    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.
