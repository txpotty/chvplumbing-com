    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Dearborn Heights

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Our descaling service in Dearborn Heights involves flushing the unit with a dedicated cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is crucial for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.
