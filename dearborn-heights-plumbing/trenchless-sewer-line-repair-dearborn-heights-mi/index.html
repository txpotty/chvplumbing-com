    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Dearborn Heights homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Dearborn Heights, MI

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when factoring in the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair. For many Dearborn Heights, MI homeowners, avoiding extensive property restoration makes trenchless the more sensible long-term choice.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Dearborn Heights, MI

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Michigan homeowners.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.
