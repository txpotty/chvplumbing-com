    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Dearborn Heights

    Slab leaks are a serious concern for homes built on concrete foundations. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Our technicians use modern, non-invasive acoustic and thermal imaging technology to accurately pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your foundation. Early detection is crucial to prevent significant structural damage and costly water waste. Once located, we provide efficient repair options, which may include direct access, rerouting or pipe bursting, always choosing the least disruptive method for your home.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Dearborn Heights

    Many homes sit on concrete slabs, so a hidden leak under the floor can run for weeks before you see it. Warm spots on tile, a running meter or a spike in your water bill are typical clues.
