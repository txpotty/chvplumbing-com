    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop using sinks, toilets and the washer. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, letting us mark the depth and location from the surface, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. We have two ways in. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.
