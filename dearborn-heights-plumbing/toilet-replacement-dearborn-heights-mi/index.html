    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Dearborn Heights

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most emergency swaps are done the same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI
