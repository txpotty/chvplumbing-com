    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Dearborn Heights

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Dearborn Heights

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Dearborn Heights

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. Our root saw cuts the mass loose, and heavy growth gets a jetter pass to clean out the fine hairs, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Dearborn Heights

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Dearborn Heights, MI

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Dearborn Heights
