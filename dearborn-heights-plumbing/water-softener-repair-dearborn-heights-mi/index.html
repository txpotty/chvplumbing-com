    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home.     Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Dearborn Heights, MI

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles.
