    Disposal Installation in Dearborn Heights, MI: The Details

    Standard Disposal Installation in Dearborn Heights

    Most households in Dearborn Heights do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Dearborn Heights

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Dearborn Heights

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Dearborn Heights homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Dearborn Heights

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. They are popular with families who have small children and people who prefer no open hopper in Dearborn Heights homes.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Dearborn Heights

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. Power comes from a licensed electrician when needed.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Dearborn Heights, MI

    The model and the power setup drive most of the cost. A simple swap on a sink that already has a switched outlet is the least expensive job, while adding power, lowering the drain or reworking a double-bowl layout adds time and shows up as its own line on the quote. The quote covers unit and labor.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
