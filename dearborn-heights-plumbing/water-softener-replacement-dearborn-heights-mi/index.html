    Softener Systems We Replace in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Softener Loop Installation in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Dearborn Heights

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Dearborn Heights, MI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Dearborn Heights

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Dearborn Heights

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.
