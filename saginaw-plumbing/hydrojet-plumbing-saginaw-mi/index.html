    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Saginaw, MI

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Saginaw

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Saginaw, MI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Saginaw
