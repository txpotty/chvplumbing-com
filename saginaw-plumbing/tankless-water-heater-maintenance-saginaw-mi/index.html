    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Saginaw

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work.     Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Saginaw

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. Hard water can lead to heavy mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Saginaw involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits.     Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Saginaw

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Saginaw is a proactive measure against costly repairs.     Error Code Diagnostics in Saginaw, MI

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing any work.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Saginaw

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.
