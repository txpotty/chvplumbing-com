    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Saginaw

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Homes around Saginaw on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
