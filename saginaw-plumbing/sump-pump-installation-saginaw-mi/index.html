    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Saginaw

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Saginaw, MI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Saginaw

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Saginaw, MI

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Saginaw, MI

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.
