    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in Saginaw

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Saginaw

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Saginaw, MI

    Whether your home in Saginaw, MI uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks confirm all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Saginaw

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Saginaw home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Saginaw, MI

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
