    We primarily install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation. Both are safe and code-compliant when installed by licensed professionals, and the choice usually depends on the application and property layout.

    The most critical sign of a gas line issue is the smell of rotten eggs, which signals a gas leak. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, visible damage to a pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. If you suspect a gas leak, evacuate immediately and call your gas provider and then us.

    Gas line installation is highly dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.

    Gas lines generally do not need frequent inspections once installed correctly, but it is good practice to have them checked during routine plumbing inspections or if you notice unusual smells or sounds.
