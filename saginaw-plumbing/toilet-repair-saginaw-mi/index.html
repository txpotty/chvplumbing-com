    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Saginaw

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Saginaw, MI

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Saginaw, MI

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Saginaw, MI

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line on every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Saginaw

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Saginaw, MI

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal.     Running Toilet Repair in Saginaw

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills.
