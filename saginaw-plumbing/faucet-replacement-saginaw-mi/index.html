    Common signs include persistent dripping or leaking even after minor repairs, visible corrosion or rust on the faucet body, reduced water pressure, difficulty turning the handles, or a wobbly faucet base. Sometimes, it is simply a style upgrade.

    Typically, a permit is not required for a direct faucet replacement unless it involves significant changes to the existing plumbing system or relocating water lines. If there are major changes, we will let you know about any necessary permitting.
