    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Saginaw, MI

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Saginaw, MI

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Saginaw, MI

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Saginaw

When the pump fails or the check valve sticks, you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, check for a crossover valve under the far sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.
