    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Saginaw, MI

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Saginaw, MI

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Saginaw

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then check on camera to see the main cause.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Saginaw, MI

    Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Water and solids sit in the dip. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, so the clog keeps returning in the same place. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Saginaw

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Saginaw

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. That beats a 2 a.m. backup.     Main Line Hydro Jetting in Saginaw

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.
