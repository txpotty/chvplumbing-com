    Sump Pump Maintenance in Farmington Hills, MI: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Farmington Hills

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Farmington Hills

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Farmington Hills, MI

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Farmington Hills

    Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Farmington Hills

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Float Switch Checks in Farmington Hills, MI

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Farmington Hills, MI

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Farmington Hills, MI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. The float rod gets checked for bends, and the stops get checked for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.
