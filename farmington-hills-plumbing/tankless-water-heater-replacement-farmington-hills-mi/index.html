    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Farmington Hills

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Water heaters work overtime through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Farmington Hills, MI

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is a favorite in long single-story ranch homes.
