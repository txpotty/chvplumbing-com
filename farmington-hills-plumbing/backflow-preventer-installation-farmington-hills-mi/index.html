    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Farmington Hills, MI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Farmington Hills, MI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Farmington Hills, MI

    Irrigation systems in Farmington Hills, MI are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Farmington Hills

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Farmington Hills, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Farmington Hills

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    While installations are usually planned, sometimes an existing backflow preventer fails suddenly, creating an urgent need for replacement in Farmington Hills.     Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Farmington Hills, MI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
