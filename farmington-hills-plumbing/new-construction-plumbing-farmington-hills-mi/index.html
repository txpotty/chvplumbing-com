    Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing in Farmington Hills

    The cost of new construction plumbing in Farmington Hills varies a lot based on the size and complexity of the project, number of bathrooms, and fixture choices.     For new construction plumbing, we primarily use PEX piping for water supply lines due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to scale build-up. For drain, waste, and vent systems, we use PVC pipes, which are corrosion-resistant and long-lasting.

    Water heaters run harder through winter because incoming water is so cold, which shortens their service life. Draining outdoor spigots in the fall and keeping heat on in vacant homes stop many of the burst pipes plumbers see each winter. We select materials and installation methods that can withstand local conditions, insulating pipes and burying lines at adequate depths where freezing is a risk. We also use materials that can handle thermal expansion and contraction, preventing leaks and bursts.

    For new construction, we typically use PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, for water supply lines due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion. For drain, waste, and vent systems, we primarily use PVC piping because it's strong, lightweight, and cost-effective.

    We typically use PEX-A for water supply because it handles heat and resists the scale and pinhole problems copper can develop with aggressive or hard water.
