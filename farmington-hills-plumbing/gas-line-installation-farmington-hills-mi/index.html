    We primarily install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation.     Safety is our top priority. We follow strict protocols including specialized tools, multiple leak tests with advanced detectors and proper ventilation. Our plumbers are trained in all safety procedures and follow all local and state codes to prevent hazards during and after the job.

    We use trusted, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety.     Gas line installation is highly dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.
