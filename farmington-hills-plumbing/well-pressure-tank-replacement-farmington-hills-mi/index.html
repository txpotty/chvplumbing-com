    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Farmington Hills

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water.     Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Farmington Hills, MI

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below.
