    The cost for frozen pipe repair in Farmington Hills can range from $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for burst pipe repair requiring extensive access or slab work.     While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, materials like PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, are more flexible and less likely to suffer bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.     The time it takes to thaw a frozen pipe varies a lot. A small, easily accessible section might thaw in 30 minutes with professional tools.     A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. Brands like Woodford and Prier make dependable models. They greatly reduce freeze damage at outdoor faucets.

    To prevent future freezing in Farmington Hills, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in attics, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Disconnect garden hoses and drain outdoor spigots.
