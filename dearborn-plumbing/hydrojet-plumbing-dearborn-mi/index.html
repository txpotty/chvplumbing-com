    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Dearborn

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Dearborn, MI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Dearborn

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Plan on two to three hours.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Dearborn

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Dearborn

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Dearborn, MI

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Spring snowmelt and high groundwater keep sump pumps running hard, so a backup pump or battery system is worth considering. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Dearborn, MI

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Low-slope lines clog more. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Dearborn

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.
