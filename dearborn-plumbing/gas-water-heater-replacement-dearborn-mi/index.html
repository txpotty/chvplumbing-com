    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dearborn

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Dearborn

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Michigan homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    No Hot Water Calls in Dearborn

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Dearborn, MI

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dearborn

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dearborn, MI

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Dearborn, MI

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Dearborn, MI

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.
