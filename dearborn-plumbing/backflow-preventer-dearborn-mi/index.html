    We service all major brands of backflow preventers, including Watts, Wilkins, Febco, Conbraco and Apollo, and Zurn. Our technicians are trained and equipped to handle many models and types, so your device receives expert attention and the right parts for repair.

    Backflow prevention is crucial because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes. These factors increase the risk of contaminated water entering the public supply, making a working backflow preventer a must.

    CHV Plumbing services all major types of backflow preventers, including Reduced Pressure Zone Assemblies, Double Check Valve Assemblies, Pressure Vacuum Breakers, and Atmospheric Vacuum Breakers. We work with leading brands like Watts, Wilkins, Febco, and Conbraco.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored water, unusual tastes or odors in your water, water leaking from the relief valve, or a sudden drop in water pressure. If you notice any of these issues, it is important to have your device inspected right away.

    An RPZ, or Reduced Pressure Zone assembly, offers the highest level of protection, suitable for high-hazard cross-connections. A DCVA, or Double Check Valve Assembly, provides moderate protection and is typically used for low to medium hazard situations, like irrigation systems.
