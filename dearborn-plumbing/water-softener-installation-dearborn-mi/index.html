    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Dearborn

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. CHV Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Dearborn home.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Dearborn, MI
