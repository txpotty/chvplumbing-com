    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Dearborn

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Dearborn

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Dearborn, MI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Dearborn

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Dearborn, MI

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Dearborn, MI

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Dearborn

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Dearborn, MI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. CHV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.
