    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Dearborn

    The cost of sewer cleaning in Dearborn can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, such as snaking or hydro jetting, and whether a camera inspection is required. More complex issues like extensive root intrusion or main line blockages will be at the higher end of this range.

    Homes with no issues may never need it. Lines with known roots or grease buildup often do well with cleaning every 12 to 24 months.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to thoroughly clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not enough to restore full pipe diameter.

    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer lines. These products are often ineffective against severe sewer clogs and can corrode pipes, especially older ones, causing more damage. They can also pose a health risk to you and our plumbers on the job.

    We use industry-leading equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our sewer cleaning work. These brands are known for their reliability and effectiveness in tackling tough blockages.

    Cleaning does not require a permit. Installing a cleanout or repairing the sewer usually does, and we handle that paperwork.

    Most standard sewer cleaning jobs can be done within 1 to 3 hours. However, more severe blockages, such as those caused by extensive tree roots or serious pipe damage, may require more time and possibly additional services.

    If our cleaning or camera inspection shows damage like cracks, collapses, or severe root intrusion that cleaning alone cannot resolve, CHV Plumbing will give you a detailed assessment and options for sewer line repair or replacement.
