Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron models last longest. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup on its own float and a high-water alarm, so a dead primary pump or a power outage does not turn into a flooded floor.

    They solve different problems. French drains handle water moving through soil. A channel drain catches surface water sheeting across concrete, like a driveway that slopes toward the garage, and many yards need one of each because the water arrives both ways during a hard storm.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Sewer section replacements and new cleanouts generally need a permit, which we handle.
