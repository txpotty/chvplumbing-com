    Copper to PEX Conversions in Dearborn

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Dearborn

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Dearborn, MI varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. In general, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates before starting any work.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Dearborn typically takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is required.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Dearborn home.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.
