    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Dearborn

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. We size for the house you have today.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Dearborn

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Dearborn, MI

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. We compare brands on that basis.

    Softener Loop Installation in Dearborn, MI

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Dearborn

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Dearborn

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Dearborn, MI

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Dearborn

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.
