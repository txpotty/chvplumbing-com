    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Dearborn

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They usually appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Dearborn

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Dearborn

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and protected.
