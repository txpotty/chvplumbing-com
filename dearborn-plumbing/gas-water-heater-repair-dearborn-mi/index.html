    Pilot Light Repair in Dearborn

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. A millivolt meter tells us in about a minute whether it is producing enough current. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Most of these jobs run about an hour.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Dearborn

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. We check pressure with a manometer. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.

    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Dearborn

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which stains sinks and clogs aerators. The tech pulls the anode, checks what metal remains and swaps it if bare core wire shows. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Dearborn home that has not started leaking from the shell. A wet bottom seam is different. That tank needs replacing.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Dearborn

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Dearborn

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. We clean the screen and the chamber. We replace any tripped thermal switch.
