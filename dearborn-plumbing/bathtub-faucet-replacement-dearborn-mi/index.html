    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Dearborn, MI

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Dearborn

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Dearborn

    Well water across much of the state tends to be harder and often carries iron, which discolors sinks and clogs aerators. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. We pull a cartridge to check.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Leak Service in Dearborn

    A broken stem can leave water running nonstop. Close the stops behind the access panel if you have them, and if you do not, shut off the main at the meter or where the line enters the house, then open a lower faucet to drain pressure. We prioritize water that will not stop. Most get fixed on the first visit.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Dearborn

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Dearborn, MI

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Dearborn

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Dearborn, MI

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.
