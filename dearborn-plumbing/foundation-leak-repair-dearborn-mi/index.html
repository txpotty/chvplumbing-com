    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Dearborn

    Modern homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation.     Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Dearborn, MI

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to find cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining.     Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Dearborn

    Many foundation leaks originate from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to move water away from your foundation. Good exterior water management is key for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Dearborn and keeping water out.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Dearborn, MI

    For homes with basements in Dearborn, MI, wall leaks can be a real concern. We offer comprehensive basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, injecting cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures stop water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Dearborn

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
