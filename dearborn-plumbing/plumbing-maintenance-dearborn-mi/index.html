    Routine Plumbing Maintenance Services in Dearborn, MI

    Preventative Leak Detection in Dearborn, MI

    Proactive leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use modern tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Newer homes mostly rely on copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion. Catching these leaks early prevents significant water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Dearborn from extensive repairs down the line.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Dearborn

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Dearborn

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Dearborn, MI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Dearborn, MI

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Dearborn

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Dearborn

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Dearborn, MI

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still smart. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.
