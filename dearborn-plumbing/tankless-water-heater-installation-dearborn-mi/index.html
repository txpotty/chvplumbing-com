    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dearborn

    Gas tankless units in Dearborn are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dearborn, MI

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Dearborn, MI homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Dearborn, MI

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. The gas line is already sized and the vent path already exists, so in many homes it is a matter of matching the connections, checking the vent for damage and hanging the new unit. Different brands vent differently. We confirm fit first.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Dearborn, MI

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dearborn

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Dearborn, MI

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Dearborn, MI homes all year long. Rinnai brings range and parts support, Navien offers built-in recirc, Noritz makes compact condensing units for tight spots, and Rheem rounds out the list. Warranty terms vary. We explain the differences.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Dearborn

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.
