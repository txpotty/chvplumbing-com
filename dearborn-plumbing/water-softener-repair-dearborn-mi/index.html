    Resin Testing and Replacement in Dearborn

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Dearborn, MI

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Dearborn

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. CHV Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Dearborn, MI

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site.
