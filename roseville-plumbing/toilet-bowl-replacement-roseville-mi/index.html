    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Roseville

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. We match the outlet and spud size. The flushometer gets reconnected and tested before the restroom reopens.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Roseville, MI

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White bowls are the easiest to find. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Roseville, MI

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Old cast iron flanges tend to rust through. A stainless repair ring fixes most of them, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Roseville

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. The bowl comes up so we can look at the flange and subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Roseville, MI

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. Loose carriers crack bowls. Flushometer valves get reconnected and tested before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Roseville, MI

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. Bowl and tank have to match. Upgrading an old toilet usually means replacing both, and we will price it that way.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Roseville

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout. We check that before you order. We coordinate with your contractor or tile crew so the flange ends up at the right height above the finished floor.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Roseville

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. For a worn gasket, a new bolt kit fixes it, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.
