    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Roseville, MI

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Roseville

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Roseville, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Roseville

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Roseville, MI

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Roseville

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We verify proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Roseville.
