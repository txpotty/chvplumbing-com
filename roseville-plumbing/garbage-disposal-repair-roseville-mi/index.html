    Garbage Disposal Problems We Repair in Roseville, MI

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Roseville, MI

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Roseville

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Roseville

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Roseville

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Roseville, MI

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Roseville

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Roseville

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Roseville

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.
