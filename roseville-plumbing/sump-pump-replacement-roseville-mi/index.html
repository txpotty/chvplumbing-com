    Choosing a Backup Battery in Roseville

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Roseville

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Roseville

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Emergency Sump Pump Swaps in Roseville, MI

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (810) 380-5918 and we will send someone. Leave the lid off, get cords off the wet floor, and run a wet vac to keep the water level below the top of the pit while you wait. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot. We also check why the old one failed.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Roseville, MI

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Roseville

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Roseville

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. The rest is simple arithmetic. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Roseville

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Nearby Sump Pump Replacement in Roseville, MI

    Local drainage matters. We look at the grading around your foundation and where the discharge line dumps, because water that comes right back toward the house keeps the pump running all night. A longer discharge run is sometimes all it takes.

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. Our sizing is based on the wettest week of the year. Average days do not flood basements.
