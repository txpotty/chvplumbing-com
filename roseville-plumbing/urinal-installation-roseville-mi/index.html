    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Roseville

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Roseville

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Roseville

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Roseville

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Roseville, MI

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Roseville, MI

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Some utilities offer rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Roseville

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Roseville, MI

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.
