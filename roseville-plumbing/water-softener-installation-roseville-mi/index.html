    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Roseville

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Roseville

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Roseville, MI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Softener Loop Installation in Roseville

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Roseville

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Roseville, MI

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Roseville

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Roseville

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. One visit covers the full install.
