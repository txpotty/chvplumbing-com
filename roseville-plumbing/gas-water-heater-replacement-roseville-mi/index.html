    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Roseville

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Roseville

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Roseville

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Roseville, MI

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Roseville, MI

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. Downtime stays short.

    No Hot Water Calls in Roseville

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.
