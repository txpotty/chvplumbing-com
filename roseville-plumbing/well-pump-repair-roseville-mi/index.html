    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Roseville

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Roseville

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Roseville, MI well.

    Submersible Pump Replacement in Roseville

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Roseville, MI

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Michigan electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Roseville

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Roseville

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. CHV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Roseville, MI well. Usually the electronics are fine.
