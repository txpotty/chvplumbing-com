    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Portage, MI

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Portage

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Portage homes.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Portage

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Portage, MI

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.     Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Portage, MI

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair.
