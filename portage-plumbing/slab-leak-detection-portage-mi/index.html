    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Portage

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.     Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Portage

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Portage, MI

    Large open floors can hide a leak. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, so we can rule out half the house before setting up the microphone.
