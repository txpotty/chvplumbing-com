    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Portage, MI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Portage

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Portage

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Portage. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CHV Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Portage, MI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Portage, MI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
