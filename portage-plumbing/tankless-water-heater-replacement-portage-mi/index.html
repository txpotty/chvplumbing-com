    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Portage, MI

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Draining outdoor spigots in the fall and keeping heat on in vacant homes stop many of the burst pipes plumbers see each winter. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Portage

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and damp basements, which can mask slow leaks. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Portage

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or seized. Most like-for-like swaps are done in four to six hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Portage, MI

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Expect a conversion to take a full day.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Portage, MI

    If you are tired of running the tap waiting for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is a favorite in long single-story ranch homes.
