    Commercial Toilet Repair in Portage

    Toilet Brands We Service in Portage

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Portage, MI

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Portage, MI

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line.     Leaky Toilet Repair in Portage

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run.
