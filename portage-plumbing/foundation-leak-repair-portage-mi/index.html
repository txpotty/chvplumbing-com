    Foundation Leak Detection in Portage

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Water Line Reroute in Portage

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Portage

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these elusive leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Portage property.
