    Finding and Fixing Slab Leaks in Portage, MI

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Portage

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Portage home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Portage, MI

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper pinholes. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Portage, MI stock fittings for each type.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Portage, MI

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Portage

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Portage homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Portage

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Portage home.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Portage

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Portage, MI

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.
