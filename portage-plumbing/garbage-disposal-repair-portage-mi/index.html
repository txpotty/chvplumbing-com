    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair in Portage

    Most disposal repairs, like clearing jams, fixing flange leaks or replacing a switch, cost $125 to $300. If replacement is needed, a new installed unit typically runs $275 to $700.

    Frequent jams, constant resets, leaks from the body, loud grinding and age over ten years are signs replacement is the smarter choice.

    The reset button protects the motor from overheating. If it trips frequently, the unit may be overloaded, jammed or failing.

    We repair all major garbage disposal brands, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, KitchenAid, Frigidaire, and more. Our technicians are trained to work on various models and can source parts for a wide range of units.

    Most repairs take 30 minutes to an hour. Replacement, if needed, typically takes one to two hours.

    You can try pressing the reset button and using the hex wrench to free a jam with the power off. Leaks, wiring and repeated failures are best handled by a plumber.

    While some minor issues like resetting the unit or clearing a simple jam with an Allen wrench can be DIY, more complex problems involving electrical components, significant leaks, or motor issues are best left to a professional. Improper repairs can lead to further damage, electrical hazards, or new leaks.

    To prevent future problems, avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, fibrous vegetables, or large bones down the disposal. Always run cold water before, during, and after use for at least 30 seconds. Regularly grind citrus peels to help clean and deodorize the unit.
