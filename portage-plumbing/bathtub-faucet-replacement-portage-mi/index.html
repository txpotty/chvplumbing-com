    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Portage, MI

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update.     Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Portage

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Portage

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Portage

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.
