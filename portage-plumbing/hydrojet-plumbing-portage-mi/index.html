    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Portage

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Root Removal Jetting in Portage

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Portage, MI

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Portage, MI

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Homes around Portage on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely.     Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Portage, MI

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Portage
