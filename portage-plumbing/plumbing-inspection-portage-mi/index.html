    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Portage, MI

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Portage, MI

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast.     Sewer Camera Inspection in Portage, MI

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Portage
