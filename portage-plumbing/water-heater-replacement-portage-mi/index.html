    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Portage, MI

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Portage, MI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Portage, MI

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Leaks only get worse.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Portage, MI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Portage

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.
