    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Novi

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Storm Drain Video Inspection in Novi

    Before and after cleaning, a video inspection is crucial for storm drains in Novi. Our cameras can travel through the drain lines, letting us precisely identify the type and location of blockages, assess pipe integrity, and confirm the results of our cleaning. This technology helps prevent unnecessary digging and gives you a precise solution, especially useful for diagnosing issues hidden underground. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals.

    Storm Drain Repair vs. Cleaning Costs in Novi, MI

    Understanding whether you need storm drain cleaning or a more extensive repair in Novi, MI can save you money. Cleaning typically costs between $200 and $600, addressing simple blockages. If cleaning does not resolve the issue, or if video inspection shows cracks, collapsed pipes, or significant root intrusion, repairs might be necessary, costing $1,000 to $5,000 or more depending on the scope. We provide clear diagnostics to help you make smart decisions.

    Catch Basin Cleaning Services in Novi

    Catch basins are vital parts of many storm drainage systems in Novi, designed to trap debris before it enters the main pipes. Over time, these basins can fill with leaves, trash, and sediment, reducing their effectiveness. Our service includes thoroughly cleaning out catch basins, removing built-up material so they work correctly and prevent blockages further down the storm drain line, especially important in areas with mature trees.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Novi

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Novi, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. CHV Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Storm Drain Cleaning Cost Versus Water Damage in Novi, MI

    A routine storm drain cleaning costs a small fraction of what it takes to dry out a flooded garage or repair a damaged slab. Many homeowners only call after water has already gotten in. Cleaning once a year, ideally before the rainy season, prevents most problems. If a pipe is crushed or disconnected, we can locate it with a camera and repair just that section. We give you clear pricing for both cleaning and repairs.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Novi, MI

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Novi, MI. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is highly effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Novi, MI

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Novi

    Residential storm drain cleaning in Novi usually runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    We recommend having your storm drains inspected and possibly cleaned once a year, especially if you have mature trees nearby.     You can clear grates and remove surface debris with a scoop. Pipes packed with silt and gravel usually need jetting and vacuum equipment.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems commonly found in residential and commercial properties.     Generally, routine storm drain cleaning does not require special permits in Novi.
