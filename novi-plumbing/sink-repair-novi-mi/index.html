    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Novi

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Novi, MI

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Novi, MI

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Novi, MI

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Novi

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Novi

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Novi

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Novi

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.
