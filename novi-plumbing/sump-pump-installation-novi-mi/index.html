    Sump Pump Replacement in Novi, MI

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Novi

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Novi

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Novi, MI

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
