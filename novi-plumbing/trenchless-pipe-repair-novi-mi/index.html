    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Novi

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They hold up in tough conditions.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Novi

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Novi yard.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Novi, MI

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Restoration changes that. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both so you can compare.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Novi

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Novi

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Pipe Bursting in Novi, MI

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.
