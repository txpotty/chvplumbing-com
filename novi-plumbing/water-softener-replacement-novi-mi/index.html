    Softener Systems We Install in Novi, MI

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Novi, MI

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Michigan homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Novi, MI

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Novi

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Novi

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Novi, MI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Novi

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Novi

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Novi

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. Installs happen before opening or after close.
