    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Skip the drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Novi, MI

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed.     Electrical Problems and Resetting in Novi

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Novi, MI

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Novi

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.
