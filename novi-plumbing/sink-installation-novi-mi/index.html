    Pedestal Sink Installation in Novi, MI

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Novi

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. We schedule around when you are open. Night installs are possible.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Novi

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Novi, MI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Novi

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Novi

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. If your sink split overnight or the drain body rusted through and dumped water into the cabinet, we can pull it, cap the lines so the rest of the house has water, and set a replacement fast. Common sizes can go in the same day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Novi, MI

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Novi, MI

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.
