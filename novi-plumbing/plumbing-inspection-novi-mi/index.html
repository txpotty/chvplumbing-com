    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Novi, MI

    Older homes in Novi often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout.     Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Novi, MI

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Novi, MI

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems.     Hard Water Damage Assessment in Novi

    Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Novi

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.
