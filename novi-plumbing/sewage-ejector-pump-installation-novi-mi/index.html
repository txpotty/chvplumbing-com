    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Novi, MI

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Novi

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. Floating basins crack floors.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Novi, MI

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Novi

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Novi

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Novi, MI

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Novi, MI

    A dead ejector pump takes every below-grade fixture out of service until it is fixed. Quit using the basement fixtures and the washer if it feeds the pit, then call (810) 380-5918 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Replacements are often quick.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Novi, MI

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.
