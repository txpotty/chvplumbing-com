    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Novi

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Novi, MI

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, and one burned element means weak heat at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    After-Hours Tankless Repair in Novi, MI

    Some tankless failures will not wait for Monday. A leak at the exchanger or a gas odor at the heater means we come out after hours in Novi.     Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Novi, MI

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake.     Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Novi, MI

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush.     Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Novi, MI

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.
