    Essential Plumbing Maintenance Services in Novi, MI

    Water Heater Maintenance in Novi

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Homes around Novi on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a softener, since mineral levels vary widely. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Novi, MI

    Heavy summer rainstorms in Michigan can overwhelm combined and storm sewers and push water back up through basement floor drains. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Novi.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Novi

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Novi, MI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Novi

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Basements are the norm, so sump pumps, drain tile and perimeter drains are a big part of residential plumbing. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Novi

    Newer homes typically use copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Novi

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Novi

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
