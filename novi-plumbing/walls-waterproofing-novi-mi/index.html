    Proven Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Novi, MI

    Basement Wall Sealing in Novi, MI

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Humid summers bring condensation on cold pipes and musty basements, which can mask slow leaks. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Novi, MI basement.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Novi, MI

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades head off costly drywall repairs.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Novi

    Many homes in Novi have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Novi, MI

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Novi

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Novi, MI

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Novi

    Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Novi, MI

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.
