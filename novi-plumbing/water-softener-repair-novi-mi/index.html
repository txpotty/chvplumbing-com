    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Novi, MI

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair generally wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Novi

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. CHV Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Novi.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Novi, MI

    Deciding whether to repair or replace your water softener can be a tough choice.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Novi

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
