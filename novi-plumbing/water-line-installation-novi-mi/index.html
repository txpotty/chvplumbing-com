    Trenchless Water Line Installation in Novi

    Water Line Pressure Testing and Code Compliance in Novi, MI

    Every new line gets a hydrostatic pressure test before the trench is closed. The line is capped and pumped above normal working pressure, then watch the gauge for any drop, because a leaking fitting is easy to fix with the pipe exposed and very hard to find once it is buried in Novi, MI. The gauge does not lie.

    Type K Copper Water Line Installation in Novi

    Type K copper is the thick-walled grade made for burial. It takes high pressure and shrugs off sharp rocks, but it costs more than plastic and can corrode in very aggressive soil, so we bed it in clean sand and add protective sleeving anywhere it passes concrete or crosses another utility in Novi. Some codes or owners prefer metal.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Novi

    Trenchless Water Line Boring in Novi

    Driveways and mature trees are good reasons to bore instead of trench. A launch pit and an exit pit are all we open, then run a boring tool under the obstacle and pull new HDPE or PEX back through the hole, so the concrete and the root zone in your Novi yard stay put. Solid rock limits boring.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Novi, MI

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Novi, MI. PEX stays covered until burial.
