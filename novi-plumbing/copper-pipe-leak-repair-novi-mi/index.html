    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Novi

    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in Novi can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility. Minor repairs, like a simple solder patch, might range from $200 to $500. More complex issues, such as a slab leak requiring specialized detection and repair, could range from $800 to $3,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial inspection.

    Most straightforward copper pipe leak repairs, such as fixing an accessible pinhole leak or a single joint, can be completed within one to four hours. More complex issues, like leaks under a slab or extensive re-piping, may take a full day or sometimes multiple days, depending on the scope of work.

    We typically use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    A repair clamp or epoxy can buy time in an emergency, but they are not permanent fixes. A plumber should cut out the damaged section and install proper fittings, especially inside walls.

    We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping, including Type K, L and M, which are commonly found in residential and commercial plumbing systems. Our technicians are skilled in repairing and integrating piping from manufacturers like Mueller Industries, Wieland and Cerro, for compatibility and quality.

    Copper pipe leaks are commonly caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and fixtures. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is usually a cost-effective long-term solution.

    For minor, isolated repairs, a permit may not be required. However, if the repair involves replacing a significant section of piping, rerouting lines or working on the main water supply, a plumbing permit from Novi may be necessary to meet local building codes.
