    Dependable Gas Water Heater Repair Services in Novi, MI

    Pilot Light Repair in Novi

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Novi

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Novi

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. While some leaks might require a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further complications in your Novi home.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Novi, MI

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Novi

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Novi, MI

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Novi, MI can range from $150 to $800, depending on the scope of the issue and the parts required.     Most repairs are completed the same day in one to two hours. Special order gas valves may take one to three business days.

    Our technicians are experienced in repairing all major gas water heater brands, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, State and Noritz. We have access to parts for a wide variety of models and manufacturers.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit.     A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Gas tank water heaters typically last 8 to 12 years. Hard water sediment and skipped maintenance can cut that.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.
