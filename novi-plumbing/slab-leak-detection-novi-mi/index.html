    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Novi

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines.     Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Novi, MI

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations.     Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Novi

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Novi home.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Novi, MI

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Leak Detection for Older Michigan Homes in Novi, MI

Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Novi, MI

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.
