    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Novi

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Novi

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Novi, MI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. CHV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.
