    Leaky Toilet Repair in Kentwood

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water each year, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Kentwood are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to accurately locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so quick repair is crucial.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Kentwood

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We carefully inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Kentwood, MI

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.
