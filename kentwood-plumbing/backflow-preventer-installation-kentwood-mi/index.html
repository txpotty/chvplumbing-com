    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Kentwood

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Kentwood, MI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Emergency Backflow Preventer Replacement in Kentwood

    While installations are usually planned, sometimes an existing backflow preventer fails suddenly, creating an urgent need for replacement in Kentwood. A compromised backflow device can lead to immediate contamination risks or code violations. CHV Plumbing offers emergency replacement service to promptly assess the situation, remove the faulty unit and install a new, code-compliant backflow preventer, restoring your water safety as fast as possible.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Kentwood, MI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Kentwood, MI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Kentwood

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Kentwood. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CHV Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in Kentwood, MI

    Commercial properties in Kentwood, MI often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and need robust backflow prevention. Restaurants, hospitals, industrial facilities and even multi-family buildings must comply with commercial plumbing codes. CHV Plumbing provides complete installation of heavy-duty backflow preventers, including large diameter RPZ assemblies, to safeguard public health and meet Michigan regulations for businesses. We handle all necessary permits and inspections.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Kentwood

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Kentwood

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.
