    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Kentwood, MI

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. The difference between two tank sizes is usually modest. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Kentwood

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, so each glass of water starts the motor. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Kentwood

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Kentwood, MI

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, and we schedule the swap around your business hours to keep the water on. Larger tanks need two people.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Kentwood

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. We shut off power to the pump first. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and plumb it back in with fresh thread sealant and new fittings. Usually a morning.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Kentwood, MI

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Kentwood

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.
