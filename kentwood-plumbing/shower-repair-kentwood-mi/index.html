    Fixing Showers in Kentwood, MI

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Kentwood

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Then call us. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Kentwood

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. An auger cuts through it. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Kentwood, MI

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Kentwood

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Kentwood

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.
