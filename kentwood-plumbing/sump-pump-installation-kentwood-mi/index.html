    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Kentwood

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Kentwood

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Kentwood

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Kentwood

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Kentwood, MI

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.
