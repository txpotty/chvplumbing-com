    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Kentwood, MI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line.
