    Finding and Fixing Slab Leaks in Kentwood, MI

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Kentwood, MI

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. We open the floor over the leak, dig to the pipe, replace the bad section and pressure test it, then compact the trench and patch the concrete so your flooring contractor can finish the surface.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. A pit next to the foundation lets the crew tunnel under the slab by hand to the pipe, with shoring as needed, so your interior floors and cabinets stay untouched. Many Kentwood, MI homes are good candidates.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Kentwood, MI

    A spot repair costs least up front. Then add the flooring. A reroute runs a bit more but avoids concrete and future leaks on that run, and a repipe fits homes with repeated leaks or aging copper everywhere. You get written prices for each.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Kentwood

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Kentwood home.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Kentwood

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Kentwood home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.
