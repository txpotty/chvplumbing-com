    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Kentwood

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Water Softener Brand Servicing in Kentwood

    CHV Plumbing has broad experience maintaining all major water softener brands, including Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Fleck, Kinetico and more. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements of each manufacturer. Whether you have an older model or a brand-new high-efficiency unit, you can trust us to provide expert maintenance that keeps your system operating smoothly in Kentwood.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Kentwood, MI

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Kentwood

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Kentwood, MI

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Kentwood, MI

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Kentwood homes.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Kentwood, MI

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Kentwood, MI.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Kentwood, MI

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.
