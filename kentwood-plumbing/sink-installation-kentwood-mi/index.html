    Vessel Sink Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter, which changes the drain height and faucet requirements.     Drop-In Sink Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Drop-in or self-rimming sinks are the most forgiving style and a good choice for laminate counters. It is also a simple way to refresh an older vanity.
