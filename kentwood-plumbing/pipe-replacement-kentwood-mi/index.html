    Long-Lasting Pipe Replacement Services in Kentwood, MI

    PEX Repiping in Kentwood

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Kentwood

    For homes with widespread pipe issues like frequent leaks, discolored water, or low water pressure, a full-home re-piping might be the best solution. We thoroughly assess your existing system, often finding old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are past their lifespan. Our team provides efficient replacement with modern, durable materials like PEX or copper, significantly upgrading your entire plumbing infrastructure. This comprehensive service in Kentwood ensures consistent water flow and eliminates future pipe-related headaches.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    Many older homes in Kentwood, MI still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion restricts water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Kentwood

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Kentwood, MI

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. CHV Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Kentwood, MI. Our plumbers are skilled in the precise soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up season after season.
