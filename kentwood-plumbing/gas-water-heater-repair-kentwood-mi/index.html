    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Kentwood

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter.     Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Kentwood

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Kentwood, MI

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. We pull the burner, brush the ports and blow out the orifice. We also confirm the orifice is drilled for the fuel you have, because a natural gas orifice on a propane heater, or the reverse, will soot up the flue and push carbon monoxide levels well past normal. You want a steady blue flame. Rust flakes on the burner tell us to look up the flue.

    Burner Tray and Venting Repair in Kentwood

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Kentwood home. A smoke pencil shows the draft.
