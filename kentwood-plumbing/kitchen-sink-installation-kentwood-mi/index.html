    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Kentwood, MI: The Details

    Composite Sink Installation in Kentwood

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Kentwood, MI

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Kentwood

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Kentwood

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Kentwood, MI

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Kentwood, MI

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Kentwood, MI

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.
