    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Kalamazoo

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Kalamazoo with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Kalamazoo

    Slab Leak Reroute in Kalamazoo

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Kalamazoo, MI
