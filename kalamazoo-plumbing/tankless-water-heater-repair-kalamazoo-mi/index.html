    Condensing Tankless Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Kalamazoo

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.
