    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Kalamazoo

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Michigan homes.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Kalamazoo

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Kalamazoo dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kalamazoo

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Michigan properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Kalamazoo.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. CHV Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.
