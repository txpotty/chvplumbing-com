    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Kalamazoo

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Kalamazoo, MI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Freeze and thaw cycles move the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
