    Buried Line Repair in Kalamazoo

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Hard freezes are routine in Michigan, and a long subzero stretch can freeze even buried water service lines in some years. Service lines fail often. We locate the leak without guessing, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line with a trenchless pull when the old pipe is beyond patching. Sewer lines get a camera.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. Vents get checked too.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. Pressure comes right back.

    Leaky Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    Small leaks raise water bills and grow mold in hidden spaces. The meter tells you. If the dial moves with every fixture off, there is a leak somewhere, and we narrow it down by isolating sections of the house before we use thermal and acoustic tools on the suspect area. Then we open one spot.

    Ceiling and Wall Leak Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    When water is pouring through a ceiling or spraying under a sink, you need help right away. Locate your shutoff.     PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Kalamazoo

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo

The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.
