    Curbless Shower Installation in Kalamazoo

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Kalamazoo

    CHV Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We make sure of proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is optimal for your new showerhead.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Kalamazoo, MI

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe more safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Kalamazoo, MI

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. CHV Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Kalamazoo.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Kalamazoo

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.
