    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Kalamazoo, MI

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Gurgling is another. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Kalamazoo

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. It takes months to close a line. Kitchen grease and bathroom hair stick to rough spots and joints until the 4 inch line narrows enough to back up, and older cast iron makes it worse by adding scale for debris to grab, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Kalamazoo

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Kalamazoo

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Sewer Line Cleaning for Rentals and HOAs in Kalamazoo

    Rentals see more clogs from wipes and grease. Turnover adds to it. We offer scheduled cleanings for landlords and property managers, and for HOAs we clean shared lines and document each building so the budget can be planned, coordinating access with tenants.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Kalamazoo, MI

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.
