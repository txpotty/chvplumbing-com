    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Kalamazoo

    The cost of sewer line cleaning in Kalamazoo, MI usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and location of the clog, and the method required, such as snaking or hydro jetting. More complex issues like extensive tree root removal or deep blockages can sometimes cost more. We provide upfront, clear pricing after a diagnostic assessment.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go many years without service.

    During our diagnostic camera inspection, if we discover that your sewer line is broken, collapsed, or severely damaged, we will tell you immediately. In such cases, cleaning might only be a temporary fix, and we would discuss options for sewer line repair or replacement, outlining all available solutions and costs.

    Older homes often have clay or cast iron, and some have Orangeburg fiber pipe. Most homes built in recent decades have ABS or PVC. Newer homes typically use copper or PEX supply lines with plastic drains, which hold up far better against corrosion.

    We use sectional and drum cable machines, hydro jetters and color camera systems with locators from trusted professional brands.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the essential step.

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts. Digging is typically only necessary if there is a severe structural issue like a collapsed pipe requiring replacement, which we would confirm with a camera inspection first.
