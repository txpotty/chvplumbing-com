    What a Tank Service Visit Covers in Kalamazoo, MI

    Electric Element Check in Kalamazoo

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. If the hot water quits halfway through a shower, the lower element is the first suspect. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Kalamazoo

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Most of those are quick fixes. Water on the floor is a different story, and the first question we ask is whether it is coming from a fitting on top or from under the jacket. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Kalamazoo

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Kalamazoo, MI

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. Past 10 to 12 years, rust at the base or a seam leak means replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Kalamazoo, MI

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Lake-sourced municipal water in Michigan is moderately hard, so scale gradually builds up in water heaters and faucets. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. If six inches of wire is bare, it is time for a new one.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Kalamazoo, MI

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. On newer FVIR models, garage dust and dryer lint can clog the flame arrestor screen at the bottom, which starves the burner and makes the pilot drop out. We vacuum it clean.
