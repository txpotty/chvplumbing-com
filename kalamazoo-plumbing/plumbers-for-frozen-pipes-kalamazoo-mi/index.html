    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Kalamazoo

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests.     Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Kalamazoo

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawl spaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens, and those spaces get nearly as cold as the outside air at night.
