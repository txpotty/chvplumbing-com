    What Goes Into a Repipe in Kalamazoo, MI

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Kalamazoo, MI

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Kalamazoo

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Kalamazoo

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Kalamazoo

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, finishing with smooth patches ready for paint. Clean finish.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Kalamazoo, MI

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Kalamazoo, MI

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.
