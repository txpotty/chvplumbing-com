    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Kalamazoo

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kalamazoo

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Kalamazoo yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Kalamazoo, MI

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Kalamazoo, MI

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Restoration changes that. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.
