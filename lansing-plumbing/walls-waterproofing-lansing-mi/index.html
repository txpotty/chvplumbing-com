    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but cannot hold back water under hydrostatic pressure.     It removes the water mold needs, so new growth slows down. It does not kill mold that is already there. Any mold already growing needs to be remediated before or alongside the waterproofing, and once the space is dry and sealed it should stay that way.
