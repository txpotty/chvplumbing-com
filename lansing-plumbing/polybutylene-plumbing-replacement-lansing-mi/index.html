    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings.     Some homeowners in Lansing, MI want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine.     Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Lansing, MI

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame.     Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Lansing

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first.     Why Polybutylene Fails in Lansing

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Homes around Lansing on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
