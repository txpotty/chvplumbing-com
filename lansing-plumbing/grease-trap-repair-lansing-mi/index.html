    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Lansing, MI

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Lansing

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Lansing, MI

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Lansing

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.
