    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Lansing

    Most homes still rely on traditional tank water heaters for their hot water needs. These units store and heat a large volume of water, making them a dependable choice for families. Our installation service includes proper sizing based on your household's demand, ensuring adequate hot water even during peak usage. We handle all connections, including gas lines or electrical wiring, and confirm correct venting to meet Lansing safety codes. Our team also disposes of your old unit responsibly, making the process hassle-free for you.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lansing, MI

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are increasingly popular in Michigan homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these highly efficient units, making sure of proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Lansing. We'll help you choose the right model for your particular needs.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Lansing, MI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Lansing

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing a lot with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a better spot.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Lansing

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a solid choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Lansing, MI

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Lansing

    We are experienced in installing all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. Our plumbers are familiar with the exact installation requirements and warranty terms for each manufacturer, ensuring your new unit is set up precisely to factory specifications for strong performance and longevity in your Lansing home.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Lansing, MI

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.
