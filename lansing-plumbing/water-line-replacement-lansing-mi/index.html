    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Lansing

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Lansing, MI

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Lansing

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. A quarter turn and the water is off.
