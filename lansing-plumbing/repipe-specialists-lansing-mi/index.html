    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Lansing, MI

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Lansing

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Lansing, MI

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Lansing, MI

    Homes around Lansing on private wells usually benefit from a water test before picking a filtration system, since mineral levels vary widely. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Lansing, MI

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Lansing

    Older commercial buildings in Lansing often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Lansing

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Soils in Michigan range from heavy clay to sandy ground, and many areas have a high water table that keeps basements damp. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Copper Repiping in Lansing, MI

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.
