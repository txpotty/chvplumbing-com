    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm.     Battery Backup System Testing in Lansing, MI

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Lansing, MI

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Lansing

    Backwater valves and well-maintained sump pumps are among the most effective defenses against flooded basements. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.
