    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Lansing

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Lansing, MI

    Many older homes in Lansing, MI still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for specific leaks in galvanized pipes where practical. Our plumbers are adept at working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for eventual replacement.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Lansing, MI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across Lansing, MI. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Lansing, MI

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Lansing

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and longevity, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage.     Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Lansing, MI

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Lansing

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Many homes in Michigan were built before the 1970s and still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Lansing is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.
