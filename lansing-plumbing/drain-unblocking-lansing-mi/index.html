    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Unblocking in Lansing

    Most drain unblocking calls in Lansing cost $125 to $300 for a single fixture. Main line blockages typically run $200 to $450, and toilet pulls to remove objects typically add $100 to $200.

    The time it takes to unblock a drain comes down to the nature and location of the clog. A simple sink or toilet clog might be cleared within an hour, while a main sewer line blockage requiring hydro jetting and camera work could take several hours. We always work steadily to minimize disruption to your home or business.

    Hydro jetting is a thorough drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often needed for stubborn clogs like heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, or extensive tree root intrusions that snaking cannot fully remove.

    In Michigan homes, common culprits for drain clogs include grease and food particles in kitchen sinks, hair and soap scum in bathroom drains, and tree root intrusion into main sewer lines. Freeze and thaw cycles shift the soil around buried pipes, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Mineral buildup from hard water can also contribute to narrowed pipes over time.

    Common reasons include a low-flow toilet with a narrow trapway, a partial blockage in the line or a venting issue. We can diagnose the cause.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors from drains, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If you notice any of these symptoms, it's best to address them quickly to prevent a full blockage.

    A plunger or hand auger can handle many simple blockages. If those do not work, or if multiple drains are blocked, call a professional.
